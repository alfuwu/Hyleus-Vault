The wind breezed gently through Heaven. Deimos' robes fluttered playfully in the open air, as if in dance.

This day, he decided, was a good day. Peaceful. Content. It was a stark contrast to how stressful and dread-filled some of his earlier days of deityhood had been. He shivered slightly at the memories of unceasing war and death. Unpleasant, those.

Nowadays, the deities had mostly ended their wars. Oh, sure, occasionally there'd be some minor skirmish between two deities in a genital-measuring contest, but nothing that ever came close to the wars that Nzlietch had caused back in the day. Well, perhaps Meloqzykes' war qualified. She had been a real doozy. Even managed to kill her other half, the rat. Now that Deimos thought about it, Lhuunkzar also came close to inciting a war on the same scale that Nzlietch had. Thankfully that mad man had been stopped before his plans had come to fruition.

Perhaps it wasn't as peaceful as he had first thought. Oh well.

As if trouble had been summoned by the very thought, one of his angels came running up to him, interrupting his tranquility. The angel, Cece, if Deimos recalled correctly, was garbed in a simple white robe, representative of his junior status and relatively young age.

"Yes?" Deimos asked, not at all miffed. He wasn't sulking. He had been alive for thousands of years! He would have plenty of time for peace and quiet later. Yes, this interruption didn't bother him in the slightest. He was too grand for that.

"Your Holy Majesty O the So-" the angel began, bowing his head so much that he almost transformed from a right angel into a right angle.

"Stop! How many time do I have to say that 'Deimos' is plenty fine?" the deity complained. Seriously, why did his angels follow his exact orders for everything *but* that? It was as though they were allergic to casualness. Even his archangels were aggravatingly polite, and they were head-and-shoulders above the regular riffraff in the hierarchy.

"Your Holy Majesty, I would never," the angel said, before continuing on with the reason why he had interrupted Deimos. "The Pristine Soul has received intel of the Cult operating within Omuaru!"

"The cult?" Deimos asked, thinking of all the different cults he had known throughout the ages. "Which one?"

"The... The Cult of Lhuunkzar, Your Holy Majesty," the angel said, shivering slightly. The angel hadn't even been alive during Lhuunkzar's time, and just his cult was enough to scare him? Angels these days. They were made of softer stock than they used to.

"Ah," Deimos said noncommittally, voicing none of his choice internal thoughts about the angel's reaction. "Well then. Send one of the archangels to investigate. Lili should do, I think. It's been a while since she visited the mortal plane. A bit of a stretch would do her good."

— **Subject**: [[Lili Soul]]
— **Location**: [[Obuandu]]'s capital city, Omuaru
— **Event**: The absorption of [[Obuandu]]'s [[Shard of Divinity]]
— **Date**: 2652 [[HUN Calendar|ME]]

————————————————

- [[Deimos]] learns of the [[Cult of Lhuunkzar]] becoming more active within Omuaru, and sends one of his archangels, [[Lili Soul]] to investigate and hopefully shut down their activities.
- [[Lili Soul|Lili]] arrives in Omuaru and communicates with the local contacts of [[the Pristine Soul]] to try to figure out what exactly is going on.
- [[Fel of the Abyss]] appears before [[Lili Soul]] and tells her what the [[Cult of Lhuunkzar]] plans to do, then vanishes.
- [[Lili Soul|Lili]] manages to track down one of the buildings the [[Cult of Lhuunkzar]] was operating within using the intel gathered from [[the Pristine Soul]] and various rumors, but they flee the premises before she can apprehend any of them. She scours the building for any trace of a document detailing what exactly the [[Cult of Lhuunkzar]] is doing, but she finds nothing on the first day of searching. She returns the next day and finds a small dossier on people with connections to the cult.
- She interrogates all of them, but they all claim to not be related to the cult or not know that they had been dealing with the cult. However, [[Lili Soul|Lili]] notices that one of them has a heavily altered [[soul]], though the traces of its tampering were covered up well. \[expert manipulative animancy]
- [[Lili Soul|Lili]] faintly senses a divine energy signature while walking about, but it quickly vanishes. \[fel]
- [[Lili Soul|Lili]] searches for an [[animancy]]-based shop in the area she sensed the divine energy signature, and finds just a single one. She uses [[Deimos]]' divine signature on her [[soul]] as grounds to search the shop's transaction logs, and notices that one person in particular bought a lot of [[animancy]] amplifiers at once. When she goes and finds that person, they show her that they haven't actually used any of those amplifiers yet. [[Lili Soul|Lili]] tells [[the Pristine Soul]] to keep tabs on them, just in case.
- [[Lili Soul|Lili]] goes back to the [[animancy]] shop and looks for correlations across time rather than all at once, and immediately finds that someone has been regularly buying powerful single-use amplifiers, the kind that would be required to perform manipulative [[animancy]].
- She tracks them down and tails them for a day, then two days, before they finally go through some sort of hidden passage. [[Lili Soul|Lili]] still knows where they are, however, due to her capability to detect [[Soul|souls]]. She follows them into the passage and finds the hideout of the cult.
- [[Lili Soul|Lili]] finds the cult just as they begin undoing the seal on [[Lhuunkzar]], and grabs the [[Shard of Divinity]] in order to stop them, despite the risks to herself as an [[angel]].
- [[Fel of the Abyss|Fel]] shows up again, grabs the [[Shard of Divinity|Shard]], and stabs [[Lili Soul|Lili]] with it.
- When [[Lili Soul|Lili]] wakes up afterwards, [[Fel of the Abyss|Fel]] hands her [[Inex]] before leaving before she attempts to cut his head off.
- [[Lili Soul|Lili]] goes into hiding because of her new status as a [[Angel|fallen angel]] and attempts to sort through all of the new emotions and sensations she was experiencing. She ends up running into [[Alfred set Tonic]], who brings her to the [[Monolithic Order]]'s headquarters within the [[Empire of Gen]].

Before Senn and her brother, Woq, lay an wretched abomination, a mar upon all that which was holy.

A perfect, unblemished sphere of reddish-purple adamantium, mixed with the holy gold of celestinium, encompassed one of the largest fragments of primordial divinity either of the twins had ever bore witness to. Concentric rings orbited the metal orb, glowing with pure gilded light.

Attached to the repugnant construct were two massive wings of crimson blood, a mockery of the sanctity of an angel.

The unholy magnum opus of the mad deity, Nzlietch.

It was called the Orgorgan, an archaic celestial term meaning *'to corrupt'*.

Senn and Woq had been fighting the creature for what must've been several hours by now, yet none of them had flagged in the slightest. How could they, when the two mortals were bordering on the edge of divinity themselves, to say nothing of the Orgorgan itself?

Around the twins lay a devastated landscape, as though Ruin Itself had seen fit to pay Heaven a visit. Entire mountains stood shattered, broken by innumerable clashes of titanic proportions. Whole forests were uprooted, the towering and imposing trees no more than twigs before the might of the combatants. Rivers were disrupted, flowing down entirely new paths and leaking over the hills like a festering, bleeding wound.

Currently, the battle had reached a standstill. Neither Senn nor Woq knew the reason, though they stayed as alert as they could, for the Orgorgan had displayed an innumerable amount of reality-defying powers and capabilities. Truly, it was a terror unlike no other the twins had faced before.

Thank the Primordials that it was the only one of its kind. Small mercy, that. Even Nzlietch appeared to have limits, sky-high though they may be.

Woq capitalized on the respite to regroup with Senn, his clothes flapping about from the immortally quick speed he possessed.

The Orgorgan's rings suddenly stilled, before rotating faster, then faster, and finally faster yet. They rotated so fast that, even to Senn's unshakeable eyes, they were naught but a blur.

Senn felt the Orgorgan's soul twist in on itself, and, fearing the worst, shouted at her brother.

"Woq! It's teleporting!" she said, desperate. They had gone through so, *so* much to finally confront the beast; there was no chance in Heaven they were going to let its slimy, slippery, spherical ass run away *now*.

As one, as they always had, the twins dashed towards the Orgorgan. Faster than a blink, nearing the speed of lightning itself, they reached the monstrosity. It attempted to dodge away from the two ascendents, but its attention was clearly occupied elsewhere as they managed to each grab one of its rings.

The rings, suddenly robbed of their gyration, started heating up rapidly. Woq tried breaking the ring he held in his grasp in twain, to no avail.

The Orgorgan's soul convulsed. A pulse emanated outwards, then imploded back inwards. Spacetime stretched around them, simultaneously turning into noodles while being crushed into paste. Light blurred, died, and reappeared in the kaleidoscopic menagerie of a dying mind hallucinating its final moments. Time spanned infinitely, yet not at all.

In a single moment that was also eternity, the two found themselves in an utterly alien landscape. The ground was barren, and the sky was a deep twilight purple. Physics held no bearing; not anymore; the ground tapered off into the void just a couple kilometers away. They were on no such thing as a "planet" anymore.

No, they were inside a different plane altogether. Something entirely separate from Heaven.

*Almost entirely separate from Heaven,* Senn amended her analysis. She could feel the subplane's tether to its mother plane.

The Orgorgan spared no time getting back into the fight. Senn could notice the reverberations of its soul, however; the Orgorgan was *confused*. It was the first emotion she'd ever sensed from it.

Clearly, it was just as astounded by the situation as she. The only material planes she knew of in the Hyleus supercluster were Heaven, Hell, and the Mortal Plane.

So where were they?

They were clearly still in the supercluster; the subplane's connection to Heaven all but confirmed that.

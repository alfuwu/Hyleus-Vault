Demons are one of the few extraplanar species to the [[mortal plane]], the other two being [[Deity|deities]] and [[Angel|angels]]. They originate from [[Hell]], one of the afterlife planes within the Hyleus megaplane cluster.

Demonic society is intensely brutalist compared to mortal societies, in part due to the immortality demons enjoy within their home plane. As almost entirely magical beings, they are rather unrestricted by mortal biology, and do not age or enjoy the capability of dying while within [[Hell]].

However, while conventional weaponry is incapable of killing a demon within [[Hell]], holy power gifted from the [[Deity|deities]] is very much capable of doing so. Generally, any being with holy power inside [[Hell]] is killed within minutes.

Demons also do age while outside of [[Hell]], albeit incredibly slowly compared to [[Human|humans]] or even longer-lived species such as the [[fae]]. Lifespans of up to 100,000 years are, while rare, not entirely uncommon.

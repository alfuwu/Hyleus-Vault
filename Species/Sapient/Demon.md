Demons are one of the few extraplanar species to the [[mortal plane]], the other two being [[Deity|deities]] and [[Angel|angels]]. They originate from [[Hell]], one of the afterlife planes within the Hyleus megaplane cluster.

Demonic society is intensely brutalist compared to mortal societies, in part due to the immortality demons enjoy within their home plane. As almost entirely magical beings, they are rather unrestricted by mortal biology, and do not age or enjoy the capability of truly dying while within [[Hell]]. Instead, when a demon perishes in [[Hell]], its [[soul]] will linger until it gathers enough [[essence]] to reconstruct a physical body.

However, while conventional weaponry is incapable of killing a demon within [[Hell]], divine power gifted from the [[Deity|deities]] is very much capable of doing so. Generally, any being with divine power inside [[Hell]] is killed within minutes due to this fact. [[Animancy]] is the only other form of magic capable of dealing permanent damage to demons within [[Hell]]; even [[Psychoticism|psychic attacks]] will slowly mend themselves.

Despite *theoretically* being capable of dealing permanent damage to a demon's [[soul]], any [[animancy]] performed upon the [[soul]] of a dead demon must fight against the plane of [[Hell]] itself, as the plane protects these [[Soul|souls]] from outside harm. [[Animancy]] is thus only viable on live demons, which makes it significantly more difficult to use.

Demons do age while outside of [[Hell]], albeit incredibly slowly compared to [[Human|humans]] or even the longer-lived mortal species such as the [[Fae]]. Lifespans of up to 30,000 years are, while rare, not entirely uncommon, though demons with such long lifespans tend to never venture out into the [[mortal plane]]. There are no alive demons older than a couple thousand years in the present day, however, due to the [[Great Hell Wars]] wiping out the majority of demon population, especially the population of the older generations.

The fae, often characterized by the infamous archfae [[Rivian]] for his widespread destruction caused during the [[Dark Era]], are a species of humanoids with an inherent connection to the [[Psychic Leyline]], affecting how fae culture and society developed. They have [[genetic magic]] in the form of wings, much like the [[Serran|serrans]], allowing them the inherent capability of flight and dominion over temperature (see seasonal fae). Most fae can only be loosely declared true [[Psychoticism|psychics]]; their connection to the [[Psychoticism|Psychic Leyline]], while much stronger than the average [[human]]'s connection, still falls short of any truly mind-bending use. These fae are typically categorized as "lesser fae".

The biological parents' connection to the [[Psychic Leyline]] influences the offspring's connection, a trait only loosely found in other sapient species. This has resulted in fae nobility and royalty possessing inordinately powerful connections to the [[Psychic Leyline]] from birth, strengthened by the countless archfae found within their ancestry. As it only influences the offspring's connection, it is not guaranteed that they possess as strong a connection to the [[Psychoticism|Leyline]] as their parents, only likely.

Fae society has four rankings: Lesser Fae, Fae, Archfae, and Royal Fae. The archfae caste consists of those who have passed the [[Gra'unak Threshold]], allowing them more diverse and powerful [[Psychoticism|psychic abilities]]. Royal Fae tend to be even stronger than archfae from birth, due to the immense concentration of [[Psychoticism|psychic]] potential within their bloodlines.

In addition to the four rankings, fae society is divided into four general quadrants, each representative of a different season of the year; Winter, Spring, Summer, and Autumn fae. In earlier historical periods, only Winter and Summer fae existed, both types acting as a counterbalance to the other when their namesake season came about, but intermingling betwixt the two resulted in the creation of the Spring and Autumn fae, with Spring fae inheriting more from their Summer fae parent (growth/heat balance), and Autumn fae inheriting more from their Winter fae parent (decay/cooling balance).

Their wings allow them to alter the temperature in their surroundings, with Winter and Autumn fae being primed towards cooling things down, and Summer and Spring fae being primed towards heating things up.

Winter fae typically have snow-white skin that easily burns in sunlight, though that isn't so much of an issue for [[Soul|souls]] that have undergone any of the stages of Transcendence. Their facial expressions tend towards neutrality or solemnity moreso than the other fae, and their voices are sharper. Their natural body odor has been likened to the smell of mint by many. Their eye color is typically blue, gray, yellow, or hazel, though predominantly blue, and they have fluffy white hair.

Spring fae are almost the exact opposite of Winter fae, being exuberant and lively, almost all fitting neatly under the term "extrovert". Their [[genetic magic]] control over temperature is more geared towards condensing water out of the air or warming/cooling down plants to optimal temperatures, making them great for agricultural work. Spring fae, like Autumn fae, can be either-or when it comes to skin tone, being equally light-colored and dark-colored. Their eye color ranges widely, being about equal for blue, green, hazel, and brown. Their hair is often curly brown, but can also be green or pink in rare occasions.

Summer fae are generally brown in skin tone, shielding their non-Transcended kin from the harsh Sun. They easily smile, but are difficult to defuse once angered. Their hair most commonly is blonde, ginger, or shining gold if the bloodline is particularly pure, and their eyes are almost always brown or hazel.

Autumn fae are closer to Winter fae than Summer fae, with their [[genetic magic]] being more geared towards cooling down and sapping heat from things. Their skin is the aforementioned equal split between white and brown, and their eye color is generally brown, hazel, or a soft red. The hair of a typical Autumn fae matches their eye color, but occasionally a fae might deviate from this pattern due to mutation within their genome.

The primary reasons fae have not enjoyed widespread political power are threefold:
- The long lifespan of the fae causes their society to be intensely conservative, taking hundreds of years to make reforms that [[Human|humans]] make in a mere dozen.
- The fae are isolationists. Little info is available to the greater public about what they are capable of, which causes many countries to mostly dismiss the fae as a threat.
- And finally, the fae are restricted by the [[Laklash]], developed in the wake of [[Vivis Zvieytymn]]'s attempted conquest of [[Drastevarde]], which prevents the fae from interfering with "lesser" societies (read: [[human]] societies) unless they have struck a deal in which the fae's intervention would be warranted.

The serrans are insectoid-like humanoid creatures. They vary in appearance, but all of them have a pair of wings and antennae. They originate from [[South Yiyico]], though they are rather populous in the modern day and can be seen just about anywhere, barring [[Pyx]] and [[Miulya]], albeit semi-rarely on continents outside of the Yiyicos. Their wings are imbued with [[genetic magic]], allowing them flight without sacrificing the utility and flexibility of a a humanoid body.

These creatures are often regarded as majestic and cute, which is further emboldened by the immensely high percentage of serrans who dedicate their lives to forging a better society for their young. This has caused many of the serran-dominant nations strife with nations that are more [[human]]-dominant, as they tend to be extremely progressive compared to [[Human|humans]].

## Notable Persons
[[Kate Askerō]] is one of the most well-known serrans to exist.

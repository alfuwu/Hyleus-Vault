Divine trees, also sometimes referred to as world trees (despite only [[Yggdrasil]] deserving that moniker) are the only genus of tree created by [[Seya]] during the [[Era of the Divine|Divine Era]]. There are approximately only a dozen divine trees left in existence, out of the original hundreds.

## Biology
Divine trees are capable of growing to be kilometers tall, and feast on [[mana]] to fuel their eternal growth, using the powerful energy to strengthen bark and structure with lattice-like supports, allowing branches to stretch hundreds of meters away from the trunk.

The leaves of the divine trees collect sunlight, yes, but their primary purpose was rerouted into collecting [[mana]] from the upper atmosphere, away from the vast majority of creatures on the ground or in the dirt, allowing the trees to easily meet their vast energy requirements to stay alive.

The existence of a powerful [[soul]] and massive size and lifespan allows them to easily become ascendent-tier beings, which further enhances the heights in which they may rise.

Deities are the ultimate lifeform within the Hyleus megaplane cluster, each one possessing vast reality-bending power. They are functionally immortal to any and everything, with the sole exceptions being the [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic magic formation]] that [[Meloqzykes]] possesses and [[Ruin]].

Deities have long influenced mortal societies, despite being restricted to [[Heaven]]. They often make use of avatars to talk with their mortal subjects, which, while powerful, are still very much mortal and have been killed several times throughout history. It can take a deity upwards of 100 years to reform their avatar after its destruction, making their death an extreme loss to any deity.

The most well-known case of avatar deicide was carried out by [[Divine Freedom]]'s penultimate battle, where the avatars of many deities and even arch deities were slaughtered. Before that point, however, the [[Divine Freedom]] had picked off many other avatars. The consequences of [[Divine Freedom]]'s actions lasted much longer than the cult itself did, carrying many widespread consequences.

Rather than being born or created, all deities were once mortal. They underwent Apotheosis, the final stage of [[soul]] transcendence. The first deities, the three primordials, were incapable of simply undergoing Apotheosis, as there existed no such thing during their time. Instead, they performed what is known today as "primordial Apotheosis," which involves harnessing the primordial [[Divinity|divine energy]] from a [[Shard of Divinity]] and imbuing it into one's own [[soul]], manually inducing Apotheosis. As [[Hyleus]] became part of the world and [[Ruin]] turned into a force of nature itself, [[M]] was the only being who was both still capable of communication and knew the method to primordial Apotheosis, but with [[M]] having been slain by [[Meloqzykes]], the path to primordial Apotheosis has been effectively sealed forever.

Deities are separated into four primary categories: **Nascent**, for newly born deities or deities who underwent Apotheosis using an extremely narrow [[Domain]], **Intermediate**, or more commonly referred to as simple "deities," for the average deity, **Arch** for the strongest deities, and **Primordial**, for the deities who achieved their deityhood via primordial Apotheosis rather than regular Apotheosis. These primordial deities often have incredibly undesirable characteristics, though in return are much more powerful than a normal deity. An example of one of the truly undesirable characteristics can be found in [[Ruin]], which became more akin to a concept rather than a true person after undergoing primordial Apotheosis.

In addition to the four primary categories, there are the deities that are referred to as **Dark Deities**. These deities possess [[Domain|Domains]] that many people regard as "evil," the most notorious of which being [[Nzlietch]]. These deities were excluded from the creation of the [[Angel|angels]], and thus, slightly miffed, set out to create their own plane, now known as [[Hell]], along with the [[Demon|demons]] that inhabit it, an act which would later start the [[Great Hell Wars]].

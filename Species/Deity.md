Deities are the ultimate lifeform within the Hyleus megaplane cluster, each one possessing vast reality-bending power. They are functionally immortal to any and everything, with the sole exceptions being the [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic magic formation]] that [[Meloqzykes]] possesses and [[Ruin]].

Deities have long influenced mortal societies, despite being restricted to [[Heaven]]. They often make use of avatars to talk with their mortal subjects, which, while powerful, are still very much mortal and have been killed several times throughout history. It can take a deity upwards of 100 years to reform their avatar after its destruction, making their death an extreme loss to any deity.

The most well-known case of avatar deicide was carried out by [[Divine Freedom]]'s penultimate battle, where the avatars of many deities and even arch deities were slaughtered. Before that point, however, the [[Divine Freedom]] had picked off many other avatars. The consequences of [[Divine Freedom]]'s actions lasted much longer than the cult itself did, carrying many widespread consequences.

A unique being is a creature that no longer fits within typical genera categories. The original [[phaawa]] were considered to be unique beings, though they have not been considered as such for many millennia at this point. These beings typically come about from modifying the [[soul]]'s blueprint layer and then forcefully establishing parity between the blueprint and the physical body with magic.

As unique beings are each as individually unique as the name suggests, it is impossible to document all the vast amounts of traits recorded unique beings have exhibited. However, for the sake of documentary rigor, some notable unique beings plus their assumed associated traits shall  be listed below.

- **[[Fel of the Abyss]]** - Possibly one of the most well-known unique beings to currently live. Little is known about the nature of whatever alterations [[Fel of the Abyss|Fel]]'s physical body has undergone, but it can be inferred that biological immortality—that is, the absence of any effects related to aging—is one of them, due to his unnaturally long lifespan.
- **[[Odi Hercul Enguh]]** - Odi Hercul Enguh is a unique being detached from the necessities of mortals. His body is constructed out of unliving materials—metal and treated wood—eliminating the necessity for sustenance, temperature regulation, and sleep. His body instead functions off pure [[mana]]—something that would disrupt many vital biological processes in a flesh-and-blood body, making it something only his body is capable of.
- **[[Miu Tonic]]** - One of the most 'unique' unique beings to ever exist, owing to her artificial [[soul]]'s structure diverging wildly from traditional [[soul]] structure at the cost of efficiency. She possesses an entire layer more within her [[soul]], which serves to protect her from all manner of [[soul]] attacks and additionally filter out harmful [[mana]]. Miu Tonic can be *technically* considered physically a [[human]], though it is more apt to define her as a unique being.

The xe'tunig are a strange species, living deep within the oceans betwixt the continents. They require specialized magical capabilities to so much as survive within the low-pressure and water-less environment on land, making their appearances in non-underwater settlements almost nonexistent.

Despite evolving almost entirely separately to the [[human]] instead of branching off from them like all other humanoid species, the xe'tunig are strikingly similar in form. Scientists believe this is due to some of the inherent advantages the [[human]] form comes with; most notably the hand and facial expressions.

## Biology
Xe'tunig are extremely often regarded as beautiful due to their uniquely alien-looking biology. Much like the Earth's jellyfish, xe'tunig do not have centralized organs, but rather a distributed organ system, barring the brain and heart.

Their biological tissue is refractive, scattering light and camouflaging their form quite well in the water. To counteract this camouflage, there are many bioluminescent glands contained within their body that may be activated with neural signals, much like [[human]] facial expressions are. Xe'tunig society has evolved around using these glands to facilitate communication with one another, and is the basis for any and all xe'tunig languages.

As the xe'tunig communicate with their bioluminescent lighting rather than through sound, they have no vocal cords and cannot speak regular land-dwelling languages without using magic to simulate the vibrations within the air. Understandably, most xe'tunig never pick up this skill; it is generally only obtained and practiced by xe'tunig ambassadors, and even then, there is a distinct kind of "accent" that this method of speaking comes with.

Instead of a centralized waste system like the intestine found in most land mammals, the xe'tunig directly excrete ammonia waste products into the water through diffusion, as many marine animals do.

For a skeleton, the xe'tunig have a cartilage-like lattice that extends throughout their body. This lattice is both flexible, semi-transparent, and durable. The lattice is very similar in visual disposition to glass crystals. This has, to many a xe'tunig's dismay, made their bodies *very* squishy and grabbable for small children.

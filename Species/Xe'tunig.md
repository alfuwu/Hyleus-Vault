The xe'tunig are a strange species, living deep within the oceans betwixt the continents. They require specialized magical capabilities to so much as survive within the low-pressure and water-less environment on land, making their appearances in non-underwater settlements almost nonexistent.

Despite evolving almost entirely separately to the [[human]] instead of branching off from them like all other humanoid species, the xe'tunig are strikingly similar in form. Scientists believe this is due to some of the inherent advantages the [[human]] form comes with; most notably the hand and facial expressions. The lack of aerodynamic form still baffles scientists, though some hypothesize that the prehistoric xe'tunig didn't require much maneuverability, leading to a suboptimal evolution that resulted in a more human appearance.

The many needs of the xe'tunig species on land has resulted in them having quite the unique-looking ambassadorial attire, wearing enchanted veils of high-pressure flowing water to maintain hydration and pressure. This veil also automatically collects and disposes of the ammonia substance excreted by the xe'tunig body.

## Language
Xe'tunig language is incredibly unique compared to other sapient species. To begin with, their "spoken" language is visual rather than auditory, but another differing aspect is their written language. This is due to the fact that paper doesn't exist in underwater environments, forcing the xe'tunig to carve any written info out onto things such as stone. This has caused xe'tunig languages to have angular scripts, similar to [[Angelic]], rather than the flowing, curvy scripts most land-based sapients utilize.

## Biology
Xe'tunig are extremely often regarded as beautiful due to their uniquely alien-looking biology. Much like the [[Earth]]’s jellyfish, xe’tunig possess a distributed organ system, barring the brain. Their circulatory fluid is pushed not by a single heart, but by a pulsing vascular network controlled by a central nexus-node near the chest (which is generally colloquially referred to as a heart). Their nervous system follows a similar design, with small clusters of neural tissue woven into their cartilage lattice, granting them extraordinary reflexes and fluid grace in the water.

Their biological tissue is refractive, scattering light and camouflaging their form quite well in the water. To counteract this camouflage, there are many bioluminescent glands contained within their body that may be activated with neural signals, much like [[human]] facial expressions are. Xe'tunig society has evolved around using these glands to facilitate communication with one another, and is the basis for any and all xe'tunig languages. This makes xe'tunig communication extremely vivid and dazzling to those who don't understand it. As the xe'tunig communicate with their bioluminescent lighting rather than through sound, they have no vocal cords and cannot speak regular land-dwelling languages without using magic to simulate the vibrations within the air. Understandably, most xe'tunig never pick up this skill; it is generally only obtained and practiced by xe'tunig ambassadors, and even then, there is a distinct kind of "accent" that this method of speaking comes with.

Instead of a centralized waste system like the intestine found in most land mammals, the xe'tunig directly excrete ammonia waste products into the water through diffusion, as many marine animals do. This is yet another facet of xe'tunig biology that requires specialized spells to deal with on land.

For a skeleton, the xe'tunig have a cartilage-like lattice that extends throughout their body. This lattice is both flexible, semi-transparent, and durable. The lattice is very similar in visual disposition to glass crystals. This has, to many a xe'tunig's dismay, made their bodies *very* squishy, and thus, in the case of small children, grabbable. Due to their skeletal softness, xe'tunig brains have evolved to be highly resilient in order to minimize long-lasting brain trauma.

Eruul'vaak are a species of sentient humanoid beings evolved specifically to survive on the [[Miulya|Miulyan]] continent. As such, they feed off heat energy, needing an almost constant exposure to high degree heat (greater than 60 degrees Celsius) to not starve. If the heat is hot enough, it'll overcome an eruul'vaak's natural resistance, though not before the eruul'vaak has eaten a significant portion of the heat energy. It is considered much more efficient to fight the eruul'vaak species with frost or cold-adjacent magic, as they naturally counter an eruul'vaak's strengths.

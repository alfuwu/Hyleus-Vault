A list of random words that I would prefer not to forget, for use in any of my stories.

### razz
*/ræz/*

*verb*
1. (informal) Tease playfully; heckle.
2. (informal) Heckle by blowing a raspberry.

### quixotic
*/kwɪkˈsɒtɪk/*

*adjective*
1. Possessing or acting with the desire to do noble and romantic deeds, without thought of realism and practicality.
2. Impulsive.
3. Like Don Quixote; romantic to extravagance; absurdly chivalric; apt to be deluded.

### diaphanous
*/dī-ăf′ə-nəs/*

*adjective*
1. Sufficiently thin or airy as to be translucent.
    a diaphanous gown; diaphanous gauze.
2. Of such fine composition as to be easily damaged or broken; delicate.
    diaphanous butterfly wings.

### rakish
*/ˈɹeɪkɪʃ/*

*adjective*
1. Resembling or given to the practices of a rake; given to a dissolute life; lewd; debauched.
2. Jaunty.
3. Nautical, having an unusual amount of rake or inclination of the masts, as a vessel. The piratical craft of former times were distinguished for their rakish build.

### debonair
*/dɛbəˈneə(ɹ)/*

*adjective*
1. (obsolete) Gracious, courteous.
2. Suave, urbane and sophisticated.
3. Charming, confident and carefully dressed.

# On The Nature of Mana
## Written by [[Lunærgen Sø-Bryu]]
## Published 0003 [[Astana Calendar|KE]]

This paper argues that [[mana]] is not solely manipulable by those blessed enough to have the [[Intrinsic Magic|engravings]], but rather something accessible to all possessed of enough [[Soul|inner mana]], using a theoretical construct dubbed a "[[Classical Spellcasting|spell matrix]]".

As was established in Talanøs' paper on the [[soul]], all beings carry within them an amount of [[mana]] in some form or other. I have observed this [[mana]] to lay almost completely inert for most mortals, serving no apparently obvious purpose. However, when examining the [[soul]] of an [[Intrinsic Magic|engraved]], this very same [[mana]] is much more alive and enegetic, especially when the [[Intrinsic Magic|engraved]] actively uses their ability.

This direct manipulation of [[mana]] by a mortal [[soul]] contradicts what we currently know of [[mana]] – that being that it is an energy form only usable by the [[Deity|deities]], and, furthermore, suggests the possibility that all mortals are capable of actively expressing their will. This contradiction demands more examination.

If [[mana]] is, as prevailing theory holds, an energy whose manipulation requires divine mediation, then the observed activity within the [[soul]] must be either a concealed act of divine intercession, a side effect caused by a concealed act of divine intercession, or a misinterperation of passive [[mana]] turbulence.

However, all three of these possibilities can be debunked. Both possibilities reliant on divine intercession cannot hold, as there is empirical evidence of individuals with [[Intrinsic Magic|engraved]] abilities under the purview of no [[deity]], and it cannot be a misinterperation of passive [[mana]] turbulence as the effect is demonstrably recreatable in every reasonable condition.

We are therefore compelled to consider the possibility of a fourth option, that being that mortals are inherently capable of manipulating this [[mana]] within them to some degree. While it is possible that this is a unique trait of the [[Intrinsic Magic|engraved]], I have not yet witnessed any evidence pointing towards that fact, and indeed, the presence of [[mana]] within regular mortal [[Soul|souls]] suggests that it is in some way manipulable by the average person.

But this leads us to a rather pressing roadblock: the [[soul]] substrate is incredibly difficult to modify, prone to egregious error, and the [[Intrinsic Magic|engravings]] upon the [[Soul|souls]] of the [[Intrinsic Magic|engraved]] are incomprehensibly complex. It is thus that I believe creating an [[Intrinsic Magic|engraving]] upon one's own [[soul]] in order to perform magic is inadvisable, which led me to the idea of a hypothetical construct that I have termed a "spell matrix".

The existence of these spell matrices is predicated upon the mortal capability to manipulate and externalize their inner [[mana]] within their [[soul]], such that it may interact with the physical and metaphysical realms.

If we were to accept that a mortal [[soul]] may direct its contained [[mana]], or, perhaps, even direct external [[mana]] directly, then the difference between an [[Intrinsic Magic|engraved]] and that of the unengraved mortal cannot lie on the capacity to manipulate [[mana]], but rather must be in the structure required to do so, the structure in question being the [[Intrinsic Magic|engraving]] upon the [[soul]].

From repeated observation during active expression, I have concluded that the [[mana]] within the [[soul]] of an [[Intrinsic Magic|engraved]] is concentrated within the [[Intrinsic Magic|engravings]] of the individual when triggered. This suggests that [[mana]], when pressed into certain configurations, causes predictable and recreatable effects. If one could learn how to manipulate their [[mana]] within their [[soul]], it is possible that they could recreate the feats of magic that the [[Intrinsic Magic|engraved]] do as effortlessly as breathing.

Thus, a spell matrix is, in its most rudimentary conception, consistent of a deliberate arrangement of [[mana]] held in patterned equilibrium in such a way as to produce an external effect. Unlike [[Intrinsic Magic|engravings]], which appear to be fixed and self-maintaining, these constructs would be provisional by nature, collapsing upon loss of concentration, or instability introduced whether via external factors or by an incorrect configuration of [[mana]].

The implications of such a facet of magic are profound. Should these spell matrices be teachable, magic would no longer be solely the providence of those blessed by birth or the [[Divine]] alone and instead becomes a discipline of structured internal architecture.

However, I must stress that this is purely theoretical and we have no current guarantee that these hypotheses are in any form correct.

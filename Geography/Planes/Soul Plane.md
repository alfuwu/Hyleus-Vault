The Soul Plane is an immaterial [[plane]] within the Hyleus supercluster. It is tightly connected to all the material [[Plane|planes]], being made up of the [[Soul|souls]] of living beings. The position of a [[soul]] within this [[plane]] roughly corresponds to the [[soul]]'s physical body in whichever material [[plane]] it currently exists in.

When a [[soul]]'s physical body perishes, its connection to the Hyleus supercluster breaks. The [[soul]] then tends to drift off into the [[Void]], becoming lost forever.

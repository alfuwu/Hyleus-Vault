The Void is the vast emptiness that pervades the [[Czite axis]]. Many creatures reside here, though all are fatal for a resident of the [[Mortal Plane]] to make contact with.

[[Lhuunkzar]] was banished and sealed deep within the void after losing in [[San'proprieno]]. It has since become a creature of the Void, and is antithetical to life itself.

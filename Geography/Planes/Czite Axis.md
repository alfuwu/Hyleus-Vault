The Czite axis, also known as the fourth spatial dimension or the multiverse, is quite unlike the other traditional, perceivable three spatial dimensions that most beings are accustomed to.

In this spatial dimension lies [[Plane|planes]]
At the base of this spatial dimension lies the mortal world as we perceive it; the physical objects, the planets, galaxies, etc. An infinitesimal distance away from this base of the spatial dimension lies the [[Soul Plane]], where the [[soul]] of every creature, past and present, exists. The [[Soul Plane]] continues much longer than the mortal world does in this fourth spatial dimension, eventually reaching the Void Horizon, whereupon once a [[soul]] crosses this threshold, its connection to its mortal coil is severed, leaving no return, and casting the [[soul]] into the infinite abyss of the Void.

The Void stretches infinitely in this fourth spatial dimension, encompassing all. Small slicing of this Void possess unique properties, such as wherever the [[Psychic Leyline]] is theorized to exist.

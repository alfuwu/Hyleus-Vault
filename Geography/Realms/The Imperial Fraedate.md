The nation of the [[fae]] is located in the archipelago of [[Annaq'Fraedar]], spanning the entire archipelago. It is governed by a council of 12 [[fae]], known as the [[Laklash]], who are elected for life and tend to represent a specific political faction on the council. This council decides upon the laws of the fae nation, and are responsible for upholding these laws when it comes to criminal [[Fae|archfae]]. For [[fae]] of a lower class than [[Fae|archfae]], a dedicated police department known as the [[Aza]] are responsible for upholding the law and order.

The most notable of laws binding the [[fae]] is the [[Gresummen Accord]], which states that to interfere with the politics and lives of mortals, [[fae]] must have an agreement made through non-coercive means with a mortal individual with a reasonable level of influence such that the amount of interference could be conceivably performed by the aforementioned individual. What decides “reasonable” in this context is ultimately up to the [[Laklash]], though generally such disputes do not tend to reach so high up in the [[fae]] hierarchy.

Until relatively recently (2402 [[HUN Calendar|ME]]), the relatively small archipelago housing the fae species, [[Annaq'Fraedar]], was a land ravaged by war. These naturally hateful and vengeful creatures put even [[Human|humans]] and their greed to shame, killing thousands of each other every month.

This everlasting war greatly prevented external politics from ever reaching the [[Annaq'Fraedar]], as just about every foreigner to arrive in the archipelago was viciously hung, killed, or otherwise publically executed. This made the archipelago an extremely isolationist realm that prevented the outside world from learning just what the fae were capable of, besides the fact that they were not colonizable (a fact rather unfortunately learnt by [[the Ckretion Empire]], back in ~800 [[HUN Calendar|ME]]).

This meant that, when the [[the Imperial Fraedate]] of the [[fae]] emerged as a unifying force under the fraed, [[Vivis Zvieytymn]], the world was immensely unprepared. The combined forces of the [[The Imperial Fraedate|Fraedate]] were nigh unstoppable by the neighboring [[human]] nations in [[Drastevarde]], allowing the fae to ravage and annex many nations. Eventually, the [[The Imperial Fraedate|Fraedate]] entered war against the [[the Trislish Republic]] and fought to a stalemate over the course of 9 years. The agreement of [[Visar-Dqijl]] resulted in the [[The Trislish Republic|Trislish]] annexing the formerly-occupied [[Drastevarde|Drastevardean]] nations known as [[Pirl]] and the [[Federative Republic of Yuati]], two countries that had bordered the [[the Trislish Republic]]. This grew the empire's influence, but the long and protracted war against the [[fae]] had severely destabilized the economy of the empire along and additionally disrupted trade routes across [[Drastevarde]] for years.

The [[fae]] would later withdraw completely from the [[Drastevarde|Drastevardean]] continent in 2415 [[HUN Calendar|ME]], when an alliance of subjugated nations was formed and overthrew local [[fae]] government along with securing many strategic points of interest from which further launched a massive offensive against the [[fae]] military from.

However, the [[fae]] had not just invaded [[Drastevarde]]. Their conquest of [[Pyx]] was relatively unhindered, though the continent was largely unvaluable to the [[fae]].

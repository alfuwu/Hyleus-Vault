The Ckretion Empire is an ancient kingdom that was been ravaged by time. It fell in the year 872 [[HUN Calendar|ME]] due to a confluence of forces, the primary of which being the emergence of the [[Magic/Evils/The Abyss|Great Evil of the Abyss]], the 3rd [[Great Evil]] directly on top of their capital city.

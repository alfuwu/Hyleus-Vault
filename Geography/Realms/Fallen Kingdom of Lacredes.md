The Kingdom of Lacredes was one of the many kingdoms that rose and fell during the [[Rebirth Era]]. The last king of Lacredes, known simply as '[[Nizo Lacredes]],' struck a deal with the [[Fae|archfae]] named [[Rivian]] in a last-ditch effort to stop the surrounding kingdoms from annexing the entirety of Lacredes.

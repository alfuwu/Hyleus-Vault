Earth is a planet located deep within the [[Soulless]] that [[Seya]] chose to be the subject of her Perfect Life Project. [[Seya]] expended a tremendous amount of [[Divinity|inner divinity]] to force the Earth into a position such that it could support life in the traditional sense.

However, due to the fact that the Earth was located within the [[Soulless]], it naturally could not support normal [[Soul|souls]]. Thus, [[Seya]] had to experiment. After first creating [[Soul|microsouls]] for simple microorganisms such as bacteria, she expended some of her [[Divinity|inner divinity]] to sustain them, she started nudging their [[Soul|souls]] to mutate. Naturally, this resulted in most of the [[Soul|microsouls]] dying, however, eventually, natural selection began to take place. Soon, [[Seya]] had a rudimentary [[Soul|microsoul]] that could sustain itself within the [[Soulless]]. This wasn't enough for [[Seya]], though, and so she continued.

[[Seya]] guided the development of life on Earth until she felt confident that the [[Soul|souls]] were strong enough to support sapient life. With the end of the dinosaurs, she began guiding the development of life into a direction that would result in [[Human|humans]] coming into existence. She tried several variations of the standard [[human]] mold before eventually settling on her original version—the homo sapiens.

The homo sapiens gradually developed on Earth, transforming the planet into a [[human]]-only society. As the Earth was not inside of one of the strands of [[mana]] permeating the universe, the [[Human|humans]] of Earth never developed any form of [[Classical Spellcasting|classical magic]]. Additionally, due to the strange nature of the Terran [[human]] [[Soul|souls]], their access to the [[Psychic Leyline]] was incredibly weak at best, and they would never develop any form of [[intrinsic magic]]. This would leave [[necromancy]] as the seeming only option left for Terran [[Human|humans]], however that, too, was off the table due to the fact that every Terran [[soul]] was incredibly resistant to any form of attack or change, including the change required for [[Soul|soul sight]].

What this essentially means is that the [[Human|humans]] of Earth had no conceivable way to access magic. Instead, they opted to tap into the power of the other fundamental forces, namely electromagnetism. The [[Human|humans]] of Earth achieved great things with electromagnetism, from the Internet (which [[Seya]] created a magical implementation of back on Hyleus) to functioning electronical devices that could handle an extreme variety of tasks.

[[Amy Zsaash Kuten]] learnt a great many things from the weak [[Psychic Leyline|Psychic Connection]] the Terran [[Human|humans]] had to the [[Psychic Leyline]], though she was forced to learn [[English]] to actually begin to understand the majority of it.

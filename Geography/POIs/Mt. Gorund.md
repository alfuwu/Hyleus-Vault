Mt. Gorund is the largest volcano in [[Miulya]]. The last time it erupted was 43 thousand years ago, which caused the entire planet to undergo a mass extinction event (for any creature incapable of feeding off heat energy) and a major ice age to occur, lasting a couple thousand years. The volcano is currently dormant, and home to several dozen species of heat-eating creatures.

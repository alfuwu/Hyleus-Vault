The Atta Jangutta is a massive, dome-like section of the planet where the laws of time were warped. It is the largest and only temporal distortion on the planet, spanning about 3 entire miles in diameter. At various points in history, people were said to have emerged from the Atta Jangutta with futuristic or historic apparel and armaments, believed to have come from different points in time by traversing through the Atta Jangutta, however the vast majority of people who enter have never been seen again.

Most of people who exited the Atta Jangutta exhibited at least minor [[authority]] over the [[Domain]] of Time, leading many to believe that being attuned to the [[Domain]] of Time is a large, or even potentially necessary, factor to exit the Atta Jangutta.

The Atta Jangutta is located in the middle of a massive crater on the northern part of the continent of [[Anto]]. There is no plant life for several miles around the Atta Jangutta.

The inside of the Atta Jangutta is a massive, labyrinthine, and twisted area, full of junk and treasure accumulated over eons that have not yet passed and eons that have already passed alike.

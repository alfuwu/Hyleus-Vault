The Stellkjurg Rainforest houses a deadly infestation known as [[the Plague]], which rots the body of any living creature it infects. It's spread through any form of contact, and forces the infected body to emit radiation to destroy any outer layers of protection another creature might have when in proximity to about 40 degree Celsius heat. [[The Plague]] was sealed away long ago by [[Vorschund von Karmelius]], though not destroyed.

The Stellkjurg Rainforest houses many monsters and creatures, deterring and barring entry to its innermost reaches, where [[the Plague]] was and still is sealed.

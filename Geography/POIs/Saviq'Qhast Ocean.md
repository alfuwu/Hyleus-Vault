The Saviq'Qhast Ocean (literally: Ocean Ocean) is a large body of water inbetwixt the continents of [[Miulya]] and [[Drastevarde]]. It is named as such due to cartographers mistaking the locals' word for "the ocean" as the proper name for the Saviq'Qhast Ocean.

## Etymology
The Saviq'Qhast Ocean was originally called the "*Svalik-ju-Qhassut*" by the indigenous population, meaning literally "water of the above", or, more contextually, "ocean of the divine". It was named as such in respect to their God, whom they believe resided beyond the edge of the Svalik-ju-Qhassut. However, this name eventually underwent simplification, transforming into the Svalik'Qhassut, and then further underwent changes when [[the Ckretion Empire]] conquered the various coastal peoples. To better fit the [[The Ckretion Empire|Ckretion]] lexicon, the name was changed to what it is now known as today, the Saviq'Qhast Ocean.

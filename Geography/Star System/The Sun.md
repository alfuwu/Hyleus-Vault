The planetary system, in which [[Geography/Star System/Planets/Hyleus|Hyleus]] resides, is a nine-planet system. The five inner planets—[[Osla]], [[Savdae]], [[Payu]], [[Geography/Star System/Planets/Hyleus|Hyleus]], and [[Geography/Star System/Planets/Ceres]]—are primarily composed of rock and metal. The four outer planets—[[Azuron]], [[Kalita]], [[Vorcas]], and [[Izraexi]]—are gaseous; [[Azuron]] and [[Kalita]] are gas giants, while [[Vorcas]] and [[Izraexi]] are ice giants.

The planetary system is also home to an asteroid belt. The [[Kepler Belt]] of asteroids lies at about 5.62 AU from <span style="color: red;">the Sun</span>, formed and constrained by resonances of [[Azuron]].

Beyond the ninth planet, the icy debris disk (known as the [[Kuiper Belt]]) stretches from 58.98 AU to 71.45 AU. Many dwarf planets lay within this disk, the most visible of which being [[Dwarf Planet 1]], at about 59 AU from <span style="color: red;">the Sun</span>.

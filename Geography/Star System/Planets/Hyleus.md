Hyleus, named such after the [[deity]] [[People/Deities/Primordial Deities/Hyleus|Hyleus]], and the fourth planet in the <span style="color: red;">solar</span> system, lies 1.560327 AU away from [[the Sun]]. It is a terrestrial planet possessed of a ferrous core, with two small natural satellites orbiting it that minorly influence tides.

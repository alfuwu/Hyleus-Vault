A continent in both the northern and southern hemispheres, though mainly in the southern hemisphere. It contains a single, massive rainforest named the [[Stellkjurg Rainforest]], along with two smaller deserts and a singular mountain range. As the home continent of the [[Human|humans]], this continent in particular has experienced a great amount of strife and warfare. The majority of the [[human]] population still resides on this continent to this day.

A continent in both the northern hemisphere. As the home continent of the [[Human|humans]], this continent in particular has experienced a great amount of strife and warfare. The majority of the [[Human]] population still resides on this continent to this day.

Due to the continent's long name, it is commonly colloquially written as "Draste".

Pyx, a continent named after the god the natives worship, is a small continent (about 4 million square miles) located directly in the middle of the [[Ghalrean Ocean]]. As the continent is protected by such a deadly natural barrier, the natives are still the dominant force on their continent, despite the relative technological and magical disparity between the Pyx natives and the rest of the world.

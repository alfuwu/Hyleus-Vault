An ancient, lost language developed on the [[Miulya]] continent. Its writing system features a set of consonants and the vowels pronounceable by [[Human|humans]], where the consonants may be combined with a vowel in a single character to create a consonant-vowel pair sound (ex: "v" & "a" to make "va"). As such, its writing system is more restricted than [[English]]'s is, but allows for cleaner words and faster writing.

The available consonants for Miulyn are BDFGHJKLMNPRSTVWYZ, where “F” and “J” can only be combined with the AEU vowels. “J” produces a “je” sound.

Its vocabulary, unlike [[English]], contains a lot of single-character words, due to the fact that a consonant and vowel can be combined into a single character.

Miulyn originates from the lost [[Ven]] language.

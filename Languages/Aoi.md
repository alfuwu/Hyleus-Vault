A language consisting solely of vowels, using enunciation to make the language legible. The lack of consonants is due to the origin of the language—in that it was created by a non-humanoid species lacking the full range of articulation the humanoid body provides. Despite the fact that it only uses vowels, the species that made the language (insert species name here) has two more vowels available for its speech than humanoid creatures do, preventing humanoid creatures from fully speaking the true language.

The vowels in the language are represented by the characters A, I, E, O, U, ϕ, and ψ. For humanoids, ϕ is pronounced as an “n” sound, while “ψ” is more of a “ks” sound (though it can also be a “ksh” sound depending on the context).

A language not developed by [[Human|humans]]—the most common language among [[eruul'vaak]], with approximately 70% of the [[eruul'vaak]] population speaking the language. The language heavily uses double vowels and awkward consonant clusters.

The language's name is pronounced “/ˈfɑːrɑːdfik/” (Faaradfighk) in its own tongue.

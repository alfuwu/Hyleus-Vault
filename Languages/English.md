The most common human-developed language. It contains very few loanwords from other languages, with those that it does take generally being related to some form of magical phenomena, and it is remarkably anomalous when compared to other human-developed languages.

The language a pronunciation of words that is hard to intuit for non-native speakers due to the many eccentricities and quirks the language contains. Its use became widespread when [[Amy Zsaash Küten]] rose to global prominence during the [[Rebirth Era]]. She and her soldiers' use of the language naturally leaked into the provinces, cities, and countries that they annexed in their play for global supremacy as the days and weeks of occupation turned to years.

The language quickly spread from there, rapidly becoming the default language at scale, before eventually being declared as the International Intermediary Language by the [[World Council]] in 2650 [[HUN Calendar|ME]], which put the language even further into the forefront of interspecies and intercontinental communication.

The language's meteoric rise to popularity left many to question where and how the language came to be, however the language seemingly is unique, with no traceable roots in any other languages known to man. It is commonly believed to be an artificial language constructed by [[Amy Zsaash Küten]] and her generals for some unknown reason, though the claim has never been verified.

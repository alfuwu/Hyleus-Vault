One of the many [[human]]-developed languages. It contains very few loanwords from other languages, with those that it does take generally being related to some form of magical phenomena, and it is remarkably anomalous when compared to other [[human]]-developed languages.

The language a pronunciation of words that is hard to intuit for non-native speakers due to the many eccentricities and quirks the language contains. Its use became widespread when [[Amy Zsaash Küten]] rose to global prominence during the [[Rebirth Era]]. She and her soldiers' use of the language naturally leaked into the provinces, cities, and countries that they annexed in their play for global supremacy as the days and weeks of occupation turned to years.

Despite the language's spread, it is still a rather minor language. Its many metaphors and breadth of phrases are the primary allure of the language, being so entertainingly different from just about every other language.

The language is seemingly unique, with no traceable roots in any other languages known to man. It is commonly believed to be an artificial language constructed by [[Amy Zsaash Küten]] and her generals, though the claim has never been verified.

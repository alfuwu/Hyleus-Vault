Xorvas the Undying was a dedicated war hero from a native [[Pyx|Pyxan]] kingdom who lived during the tail end of the [[Rebirth Era]]. He was the first host of [[the Bloodied Throne]], kickstarting its inhumane terror. During the many battles he had fought in, he had accumulated several permanent wounds both on his body and [[soul]], which were only further compounded when his brief excursions into [[the Spiral]] caused micro-spirals to develop within his bloodstream, slowly sapping his [[Vital Magic|lifeblood]] and [[mana]].

This confluence of factors led Xorvas to pursue a solution, such that he might continue to live on in service to his people and continue the good fight.

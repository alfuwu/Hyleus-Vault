Lunærgen Sø-Bryu, perhaps one of the most influential figures in recorded history, is widely regarded as the father of modern magic. His discovery was so influential that it transcended nearly all boundaries between species, even isolationist and xenophobic creatures.

He was the man who paved the way for modern magic, discovering the first form of [[Classical Spellcasting|classical magic]]: spell matrices. Ritual magic was discovered by an unknown cult, estimated to be about 50 years after Lunærgen Sø-Bryu's discovery, and alchemy had been "discovered" for thousands of years, but was truly revolutionized by [[Fürrdegrânne]]'s applications of the scientific method to the field some 400 years after Lunærgen Sø-Bryu's discovery. Artifice is a comparatively modern invention, owing its discovery to [[Dyarvh Cele Talamos]] in the year 2105 [[Astana Calendar|ME]], who created the initial runic alphabet.

Lunærgen Sø-Bryu was a rather secluded, secular man.

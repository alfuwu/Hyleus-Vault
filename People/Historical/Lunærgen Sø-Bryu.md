Lunærgen Sø-Bryu, perhaps one of the most influential figures in recorded history, is widely regarded as the father of modern magic. His discovery was so influential that it transcended nearly all boundaries between species, even isolationist and xenophobic creatures.

He discovered two of the four forms of [[Classical Spellcasting|classical magic]]: spell matrices and artifice. Ritual magic was discovered by an unknown cult, estimated to be about 50 years after Lunærgen Sø-Bryu’s discovery, and alchemy was discovered by [[Fürrdegrânne]] some 20 years after ritual magic.

Lunærgen Sø-Bryu was a rather secluded, secular man.

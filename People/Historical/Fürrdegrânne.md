Fürrdegrânne is another very influential figure, hailing from the [[Šapfōrāk]] people and the [[xe'tunig]] species. Inspired by [[Lunærgen Sø-Bryu]]’s revolutionary work, she pursued other avenues of magical enlightenment. Eventually, she founded the practice of alchemy, the culmination of her life's work.

Alchemy has allowed people to do many things, such as creating magical liquids packed full of a specially curated form of vital energy (generally troll blood) to allow the body to heal from grievous injuries, or the transmutation of certain materials to create magical alternatives, etc.

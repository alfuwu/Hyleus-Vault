Fürrdegrânne is another very influential figure, hailing from the [[Šapfōrāk]] people and the [[ne'zuni]] species. Inspired by [[Lunærgen Sø-Bryu]]'s brilliant work, she pursued other avenues of magical enlightenment. She sought to harness the capabilities of ancient [[Classical Spellcasting|alchemy]], to make it more precise, safer, cheaper, and predictable, to which she succeeded, resulting in the practice of [[Classical Spellcasting|alchemy]] spreading far and wide. The revolutionization of what would go on to be one of the four cornerstones of [[Classical Spellcasting|classic magic]] was an even greater feat than it may seem at first glance, taking into account the tedious setup Fürrdegrânne was forced to undergo as a result of doing [[Classical Spellcasting|alchemy]] underwater and in a high pressure environment.

[[Classical Spellcasting|Alchemy]] has allowed people to do many things, such as creating magical liquids packed full of a specially curated form of [[Vital Magic|vital energy]] (generally ...) to allow the body to heal from grievous injuries (though the user must know how to direct and utilize these [[Vital Magic|vital energies]]), or the transmutation of certain materials to create magical alternatives, etc.

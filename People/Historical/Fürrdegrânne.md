Fürrdegrânne is another very influential figure, hailing from the [[Šapfōrāk]] people and the [[Xe'tunig]] species. Inspired by [[Lunærgen Sø-Bryu]]'s revolutionary work, she pursued other avenues of magical enlightenment. Eventually, she founded the practice of [[Classical Spellcasting|alchemy]], the culmination of her life's work. The creation of what would go on to be one of the four cornerstones of [[Classical Spellcasting|classic magic]] was an even greater feat than it may seem at first glance, taking into account the tedious setup Fürrdegrânne was forced to undergo as a result of doing alchemy underwater and in a high pressure environment.

Alchemy has allowed people to do many things, such as creating magical liquids packed full of a specially curated form of vital energy (generally troll blood) to allow the body to heal from grievous injuries, or the transmutation of certain materials to create magical alternatives, etc.

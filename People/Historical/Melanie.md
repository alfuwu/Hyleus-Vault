Melanie (born 06/13/2608 [[HUN Calendar|ME]], died 09/06/2653 [[HUN Calendar|ME]])

### Physical Description
Melanie was above-average female [[fae]] height, standing at a crisp 4′8″ (146 cm).

### Personality
Melanie was an unstable, rather rude person.

### Ancestry


### Childhood
Melanie was a [[fae]] born with the gift of [[intrinsic magic]], yet also the curse of an unusually weak connection to the [[Psychoticism|Psychic Leyline]] compared to the median [[fae]]. Her weak [[Psychoticism|psychic]] capabilities crippled any and all chances she would ever get to cross the [[Gra'unak Threshold]] and become an [[Fae|archfae]].

### Affiliations
Melanie had high-up connections with the [[Monolithic Order]] until rather recently, when she got into a fight with two of the four Monoliths. She was then excommunicated from the Order, though [[Ezkli Reday]] kept up contact with Melanie until her untimely death.

Melanie had no other affiliations with any organization.

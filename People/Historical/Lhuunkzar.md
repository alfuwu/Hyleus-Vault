Lhuunkzar, also known as the World Devourer, is the primary cause of the catastrophic battle of [[San'proprieno]]. Following those events, several deities, including one [[Deimos]] Himself, banded together to seal Lhuunkzar within a (relatively) stable material [[Plane|subplane]]. However, due to Lhuunkzar's ascendant [[soul]] being too powerful, the [[Plane|subplane]]'s connection was unable to be severed from its mother plane. This has left Lhuunkzar with a connection to the Hyleus supercluster, which he uses to influence the minds of mortals via the [[Psychic Leyline]], forcing them into doing his bidding and creating the [[Cult of Lhuunkzar]].

His time spent in such close proximity to the [[Void]] has drastically altered Lhuunkzar's physical and mental faculties. While he was once part of the humanoid sapient races, he now more closely resembles a gargantuan worm. The eldritch influence of the [[Void]] has seeped into his very [[soul]] over the centuries, warping him into something utterly alien to what he once was. It would be more accurate to define Lhuunkzar as one of the many inhabitants of the [[Void]] rather than a flesh-and-blood sapient creature at this point, and the consequences of letting such an entity back into one of the material [[Plane|planes]] inside of the Hyleus supercluster would almost certainly have dire ramifications.

The [[Deity|deities]] have closely observed Lhuunkzar's transformation, and many are growing concerned with the increasing instability of his prison [[Plane|subplane]] caused by his mere presence.

During his tenure in the [[mortal plane]], Lhuunkzar was a master [[Classical Spellcasting|ritualist]].

Lhuunkzar, also known as the world devourer, is the primary cause of the catastrophic battle of [[San'proprieno]]. Following those events, several deities, including one [[Deimos]] Himself, banded together to seal Lhuunkzar within a (relatively) stable material [[Plane|subplane]]. However; as Lhuunkzar's ascendent [[soul]] was too powerful, the [[Plane|subplane]]'s connection was unable to be severed from its mother plane. This has left Lhuunkzar with a connection to the Hyleus Supercluster, which he uses to influence the minds of mortals via the [[Psychic Leyline]], forcing them into doing his bidding and creating the [[Cult of Lhuunkzar]].

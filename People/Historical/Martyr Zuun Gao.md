Martyr Zuun Gao is one of the most infamous psychics in written history. She began her journey when she discovered her innate connection to the [[Psychic Leyline]] at just 5 years old (approximately 3 years before the average discovery rate of one's connection to the [[Psychic Leyline]]), displaying extraordinary cognitive abilities and abstract reasoning. Her talent led her to unconsciously feel others' emotions and their thoughts, making her appear incredibly insightful to all who met her.

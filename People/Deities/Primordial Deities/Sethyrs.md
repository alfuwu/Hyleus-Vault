Sethyrs is the mother of all other deities, barring her two sibling primordials, [[People/Deities/Primordial Deities/Hyleus|Hyleus]] and [[Ruin]]. As the [[deity]] of Apotheosis, she was required for mortals to undergo regular Apotheosis. Due to her death in [[Divine Freedom]]'s final push, mortals are incapable of performing regular Apotheosis, and, if they still wish to achieve deityhood, must instead use the much more dangerous and volatile method of primordial Apotheosis.

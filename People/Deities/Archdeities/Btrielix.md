Btrielix was the one and only [[deity]] capable of manipulating spacetime. There have been none since, and none before. He fell during the [[Great Hell Wars]], though not to [[Nzlietch]], but rather [[Uentzi]] by way of assassination, just about the only way Btrielix could .

In technicality, Btrielix is not truly "dead," as He did many things in the future, though most of which have already come to pass. This also meant that Btrielix knew of His impending death for a long time, as many have told his future avatars of His fate.

Btrielix was one of the strongest archdeities to ever live.

Jik, known as the gremlin [[deity]], used the [[Divinity|divine]] capability to create [[Soul|souls]] to His own advantage, creating [[Angel|angels]] for the sole purpose of praying to Him to give Him [[essence]]. The effect rapidly escalated as He ordered His [[Angel|angels]] to traverse the [[mortal plane]] and collect as much [[essence]] as they could, thus allowing Him to create more and more [[angel]] [[Soul|souls]].

The other [[Deity|deities]] found this abuse of [[Angel|angels]] to be of poor taste, and so they gave Him an ultimatum; quit creating [[Angel|angels]] for the purpose of praying to Him, or be barred from making any [[Angel|angels]] ever again. [[Angel|Angels]], despite being completely subservient towards their patron [[deity]], were never meant to exist solely to create more [[Angel|angels]], and [[Deimos]] in particular was rather vocal about his work being used in such a way.

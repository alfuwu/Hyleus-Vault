Jik, known as the gremlin [[deity]], used the [[Divinity|divine]] capability to create [[Soul|souls]] to His own advantage, creating [[Angel|angels]] for the sole purpose of praying to Him to give Him [[essence]]. The effect rapidly escalated as He ordered His [[Angel|angels]] to traverse the [[Mortal Plane]] and collect as much [[essence]] as they could, allowing Him to create more and more [[angel]] [[Soul|souls]].

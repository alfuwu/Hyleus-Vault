Hakscles is the [[Deity]] who sealed [[Meloqzykes]] deep within the [[Soulless]], where she was left to rot on a barren rock floating in space. However, the [[Soulless]], paired with [[Meloqzykes]]' indomitable willpower, wore down her cage over time; even a [[Deity]]'s [[Divinity]] is not above entropy.

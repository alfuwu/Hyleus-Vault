Ilythra Joculen (born 02/10/2638 [[HUN Calendar|ME]]), heir to the throne of the [[Island Nation of Joculen]] before [[the Fractured|the Fracturing]], is a willful human

### Physical Description
Ilythra Joculen possesses an extremely fit body, weighing in at 194.3lbs (88.13kg). She has worked out since she was a small child, using her free time to do exercises, weapons practice, and a whole host of other outdoors hobbies. She's 5′7″ feet (170 cm) tall.

Despite her body's muscles, her figure retains its feminine charms. She has pale white skin, icy blue eyes, and silver hair that glistens softly under moonlight.

Her face is marred by a pink scar, trailing all the way from the top of her forehead's left side to the top of her right cheek, passing over her eye. As a result, her right eyelid is more difficult to blink than her left eyelid, and her right eye's iris has since discolored into a dull gray. She can still see out of her right eye, though she is still subtly visually impaired. The scar was granted unto her during [[The Fractured|the Fracturing]].

Odi Hercul Enguh (born 04/08/365 [[HUN Calendar|ME]], remade 16/07/398 [[HUN Calendar|ME]]) has lived a tragic life. A man, once [[human]], forced via [[Animancy|animantic]] rituals into the body of a mechanical construct, becoming a [[unique being]].

Before the [[Animancy|animantic]] ritual that completely upended his life, Odi Hercul Enguh had been a farmer, working the fields in [[the Ckretion Empire]]. He had had a wife and children, though he never learned of their fates.

As a construct, he no longer subsists off of regular foods, instead directly refining [[mana]] into usable energy to power his body. Additionally, as Odi was freed from the complex biological machinery that makes up flesh-and-blood bodies, he will never die of old age so long as he takes proper care of his body.

Odi killed the [[Animancy|necromancer]] who had attempted to force his [[soul]] into submission as soon as he was able, gaining freedom at the expense of meaning. Suddenly unsure to do with his nigh-infinite lifespan, Odi wandered the world, a feat made possible only by his utter lack for any survival necessities.

Later in his long life, Odi Hercul Enguh found purpose in the service of the [[Island Nation of Joculen|Joculen]] royal family.

### Physical Description
Odi Hercul Enguh is massive, unrestricted by biological and evolutionary constraints, clocking it at about 6′6″ (198 cm). He weighs 302 lbs in [[Earth]]'s gravity (137 kg).

The majority of Odi's physical body is made out of a strong and dense [[mithril]]-tungsten alloy in conjunction with treated majeveny (*Rysalis omentum*) wood. The remaining portion of his body was constructed with various required materials in order to get the body into a properly operatable state. Due to this, his body is particularly susceptible to water, though the weakness is minor at best due to the [[mana]] strengthening atomic bonds within his body.

### Personality
Odi Hercul Enguh used to be a rather basic man, living his life as a regular citizen would. That was all completely changed after he was forced into the body of a construct, where he became filled with rage and hate at the world and, in particular, his captors.

Lacking a proper organic brain, Odi Hercul Enguh does not feel fear, sexually aroused, or have hormonally-based mood swings. His self-preservation instinct is minor at best. This lack of consideration for himself is primarily what allowed Odi Hercul Enguh to so utterly annihilate his captors when the perfect opportunity presented itself.

With the object of his obsession destroyed, Odi Hercul Enguh's personality collapsed into a depressive mess, whereupon he made the decision to wander the great lands of Hyleus. These wanderings helped cool off the burning hate he still held for the world, allowing him to think and later leading him to adopt an ascetic monk lifestyle.

He travelled for years on end, a feat only made possible by his lack of a need for sleep, water, food, or shelter, and met many strange peoples he would have never had imagined meeting as a farmer. These interactions broadened Odi's horizon, made him more accepting and cooled the embers of hate burning within his soul.

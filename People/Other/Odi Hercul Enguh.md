Odi Hercul Enguh (born 04/08/365 [[HUN Calendar|ME]], remade 16/07/398 [[HUN Calendar|ME]]) is a tragic being. A man once human forced via [[Animancy|animantic]] rituals into a mechanical construct, Odi Hercul Enguh found purpose in the service of the Joculen royal family.

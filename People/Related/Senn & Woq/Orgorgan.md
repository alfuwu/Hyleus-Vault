The Orgorgan, a divine artifact from the [[Era of the Divine|Divine Era]], is truthfully more akin to an actual person than an artifact. The Orgorgan possesses a true soul and a body, though its body takes the form of a featureless white sphere with a blood-red pair of angel wings attached to it. Several rings of pure, glowing gold orbit the Orgorgan's body.

Upon the Orgorgan's soul lay several dozen [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic formations]], placed there by its creator deity, [[Nzlietch]].

The Orgorgan's body is made out of a [[Shard of Divinity]] encompassed by a composite metallic alloy forged of [[adamantium]] and [[celestium]].

The Orgorgan was sealed inside of a proto-plane in the year 2203 [[HUN Calendar|KE]], alongside [[Senn]] and [[Woq]]. The Orgorgan has been engaged in an everlasting battle with the two siblings ever since.

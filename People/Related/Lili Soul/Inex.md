Inex (the name given to the blade by [[Lili Soul]]), full name Inexaumos, is an extremely powerful artifact forged purely of [[ksol]] and [[adamantium]]. [[Fel of the Abyss]] used his spacetime-warping [[intrinsic magic]] to store a, for all intents and purposes, infinite amount of mana within the blade. The blade also seems to generate its own mana over time, though the amount it generates is but a drop in the bucket compared to what it already contains.

He later gave the blade to [[Lili Soul]] shortly after she became a [[Angel|fallen angel]], only stating that he "did not have a use for the blade anymore." [[Lili Soul|Lili]] has been the wielder of the blade ever since. It is unknown why [[Fel of the Abyss]] chose [[Lili Soul]] to be the next wielder of the blade.

The blade itself is shaped like an ornate clock hand, completely black. The clock hand converges at the base to form a handle, wrapped in what would be basic leather had it not been treated with an absurd amount of mana. It can be observed to be leaking trace amounts of spacetime-attuned mana at all times.

## Functions
Inex holds the capability to restore the user to any number of previous positions they've ever visited. It can transport the user across things ranging from planar barriers to archmage-level magical defenses with no issues due to the abundance of spacetime mana contained within the blade.

Additionally, the blade can create holes to the [[Void]]. It is highly recommended that you do not enter one of these holes should you see one, as the [[Void]] is very unfriendly to mortal life. It is still unrecommended to enter if you are immortal.

## Origin
Inex was created by [[Fel of the Abyss|Fel]] shortly he escaped from [[the Abyss]]. Several official records from meetings upon which [[Fel of the Abyss|Fel]] intruded on paint a picture of him lamenting the fact that he had not made the blade sooner, while he was still inside of [[the Abyss]]. He goes on quote to have said, rather plainly: "It would have made my life so much easier."

Lzywausya, heirloom of the Reday family, is a sword forged solely out of an [[orichalcum]]-[[mithril]] alloy known as ljimidja. The weapon is one of the first known anti-mage weapons to ever exist, and is capable of dispelling extremely high-level spells. Simply placing the sword near most low-level magical constructs is enough to disrupt their [[mana]] flow and short-circuit them.

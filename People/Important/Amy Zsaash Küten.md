Amy Zsaash Küten (born 19/08/2587 [[Astana Calendar|ME]]) , nickname the Conqueror, is an immensely powerful [[Psychoticism|psychic]] known for her strategic and combat prowess. She rose to prominence during the [[Rebirth Era]], where she and her armies conquered multiple kingdoms after performing a military coup to take control of their own country, the [[Republic of Irigr|Irigr Kingdom]].

Amy was one of the first to arrive at [[the Spiral]] after it awakened, and was pivotal in stemming the casualties. Eyewitness reports claim that she even came to blows with the [[Chronovore]] itself for an extremely limited duration of time.

### Physical Description
Amy Küten is tall for a [[human]] woman, standing at about 5′9″ (175 cm). She has olive-colored skin and rich, purple-colored hair, a characteristic feature of her ancestry.

### Personality
Amy is ambitious, driven person with an extremely strong moral compass. She will do what she thinks is right and just regardless of consequence to herself or her person; in other words, she is a true hero.

### Childhood
Amy Zsaash Küten was perhaps the youngest ever [[Psychoticism|psychic]], developing her connection to the [[Psychoticism|Psychic Leyline]] at the ripe age of 3 years old. This connection heavily influenced her young mind, leading her to learn the [[English]] language from psychic emanations from [[Earth]]. She would later use [[English]] to communicate with her men in order to deter eavesdroppers, something that was only logistically feasible due to her ability to transfer the key parts of the language directly into the minds of her men.

Amy's original name was Amine Zsaash Küten, but her child self butchered her name into Amy due to her connection to the [[Psychic Leyline]] and the prevalence of the name "Amy" on [[Earth]], and she never bothered correcting herself in her later years.

### Youth
Amy's connection to the [[Psychic Leyline]], and, in particular, her unusually strong ability to observe psychic emanations from [[Earth]], allowed her to easily breeze through academics during her youth. She was consistently top of her class in all subjects, barring world subjects such as geography and history.

### Wars
When the [[Rebirth Era]] began in earnest, 19-year-old Amy saw an opportunity to do what she had wanted to do since she was just a young girl: overthrow the government. Her home country's government was positively rotten, and controlled by an absolute monarch, denying the people any voice. Thus, Amy's first war began against her own country. She rallied rebels and likeminded individuals under her banner, and used her education along with her knowledge stolen from [[Earth]] to devise tactics and stratagems to utterly trounce her opposition. Her coup d'état ended, rather anticlimactically, with her slaying the monarch within his bedchambers. She then reformed the government, instituting a presidential republic in lieu of an absolute monarchy.

### Affiliations
Amy Zsaash Küten is primarily affiliated with her country, the [[Republic of Irigr]].

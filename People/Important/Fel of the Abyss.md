Fel of the Abyss is the only recorded living being to have escaped ground zero of [[the Abyss]]. His species is unknown and does not match any known sapient species, suggesting that he may be a unique being. His past life is unknown, though it is presumed he was a resident of [[the Ckretion Empire]], possibly even a ~~[[human]]~~.

Mr. Fel is known to possess some form of unique magic, likely [[intrinsic magic]], and some form of connection to the [[Void]].

### Personality
Fel of the Abyss is incredibly eccentric, violent, enigmatic, and unstable. It is presumed that he is insane.

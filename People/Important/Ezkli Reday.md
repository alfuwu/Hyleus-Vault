Ezkli Reday, twin sibling of [[Tarik Reday]], is a famed sorceress. She is quite talented in [[classical spellcasting]], specifically spell matrices, though her real gift lie in the [[intrinsic magic]] she and her twin possess.

Her [[intrinsic magic]] is unique in that it is not unique solely to her, but rather the [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic magic formation]] is present on both her and her twin brother's [[Soul|souls]].

Ezkli Reday is the lover of [[Alfred Tonic]].

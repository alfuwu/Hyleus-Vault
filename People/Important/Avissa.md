The High Empress Avissa (born 04/05/3463 [[Astana Calendar|KE]]) is the ruler over the plane of [[Hell]]. She is one of the strongest and oldest [[Demon|demons]] to still live, having experienced and partaken in the [[Great Hell Wars]] firsthand.

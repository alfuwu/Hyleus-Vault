Miu set Tonic (made 24/11/2652 [[Astana Calendar|ME]]), daughter of [[Alfred set Tonic]], is the first and only true [[soul]] constructed by a being that isn't a [[deity]]. Rather than develop during pregnancy, Miu's [[soul]] was constructed from the ground up by the efforts of [[Alfred set Tonic]], who treats her as his own daughter. [[Ezkli Reday]] additionally treats Miu as such.

Due to her [[soul]] being constructed from scratch, [[Alfred set Tonic]] was able to make many tweaks and optimizations, though even so, it remains "supbar" compared to a natural [[soul]]. One of such tweaks is the addition of a layer over the surface layer, called "the bulwark" by [[Alfred set Tonic|Alfred]]. This layer constantly consumes a small portion of [[mana]] from the well which it uses to project specialized defensive [[animancy]], and additionally acts as a buffer that offensive [[animancy]] must pierce before reaching the surface layer. This makes Miu functionally immune to the [[Animancy|animantic]] arts. Additionally, the bulwark filters out malicious [[mana]] automatically, protecting Miu's inner soul from the life-threatening diseases which may emerge from [[mana]] infections.

Her [[soul]] was constructed by [[Alfred set Tonic|Alfred]] after much careful research into how [[Nzlietch]] created the [[Demon|demons]], studying the magical and physical makeup of a [[soul]], and uncountable hours experimenting. ### Personality
Miu has an unnaturally energetic and infectious countenance, tending to look at things with unbridled optimism. Her extreme personality is very likely a quirk of her constructed [[soul]].

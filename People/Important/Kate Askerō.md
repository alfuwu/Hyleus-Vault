Kate Askerō (born 26/05/2619 [[HUN Calendar|ME]]), a [[serran]] born to , ...

She possesses [[intrinsic magic]]. The [[domain]] of her [[intrinsic magic]] appears to be related to energy conversion. The [[intrinsic magic|formation]] allows Kate to choose any energy acting upon or around her body and convert it into any other kind of energy, making her functionally invincible. She cannot be physically outmatched, as she can simply annul any kinetic energy. She cannot be burnt to death, as she can simply convert the heat energy into kinetic energy or [[mana]]. Even for harmful material compounds such as acid, she can simply cool down the substance so much that it lacks the energy to react with anything. Her only weaknesses are areas lacking in energy, such as vacuums or extremely cold environments.

Her capability with her [[intrinsic magic]] has been shown to be directly reliant on the amount of "willpower", as unquantifiable as that is, she has.

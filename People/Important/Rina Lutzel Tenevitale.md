Rina Lutzel Tenevitale (born 29/06/2637 [[Astana Calendar|ME]]) is the third in line to the throne of [[Tenevitale]], daughter of [[]] and [[]]. She is the twin sister of [[Rainrai Heima Tenevitale]], and younger sister of [[]].

Rina Lutzel Tenevitale's [[intrinsic magic]] comes in the form of—perhaps fittingly for her species—consumption. She is able to consume small portions of another's [[soul]], stealing their [[essence]] and optimizing her own [[soul]]'s blueprint; not unlike [[the Bloodied Throne]], though in a much less vicious and heavy-handed a manner. Those who are proficient in [[animancy]] have been shown to be more resilient to this process, though most aren't entirely immune either.

Her possession of [[intrinsic magic]] has led some Tenevitalean nobility to lobby for putting her on the throne rather than her older siblings, labelling her as a sign of divine favor. These nobles are a minority, however, and Rina herself has expressed disinterest in the throne.

### Physical Description
Rina Lutzel Tenevitale is rather short, being approximately 4′8″ (147 cm). She has long, grayish-white hair, and modestly tanned skin. She typically dresses in white, with the [[ksol]] necklace she was given as a child contrasting nicely, and likes the aesthetic fake wings provide her.

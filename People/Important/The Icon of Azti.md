### Trivia
Icon has over 15 million followers on the popular social media website [[Sanna]].

In one instance, a poll on [[Sanna]] showed that 68% of people on the platform would vote for Icon if she ran for presidency. The poll, at the time of closing, had a total of 728,440 votes. Naturally, this data is somewhat skewed due to both the fact that the primary audience of the poll being Icon's own followers and thus biased towards her, and that just because someone clicks "Yes" on a poll online doesn't mean they would actually do as they said when push comes to shove.

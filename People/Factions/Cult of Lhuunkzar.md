The Cult of [[Lhuunkzar]] (AKA the Cult of the [[Lhuunkzar|World Devourer]]) is a cult that seeks to bring about the second coming of the "[[Lhuunkzar|World Devourer]]"—a massive, worm-like creature currently sealed within the [[Void]].

This cult has followers worldwide, however the majority are centered upon the continent of [[Drastevarde]].

The group is mostly comprised of individuals whose [[Lhuunkzar]]'s vile influence has tainted, however it also contains few members who joined of their own free volition. Those who are tainted believe that they are doing things of their own accord, and are incapable of questioning any contradictions, however obvious they may be. The most notable thing that members of the cult do not acknowledge is how they possess their mastery of rituals, despite the fact that the majority of the cult's following used to be farmers or part of other less academically-inclined professions.

The Cult of [[Lhuunkzar]] (AKA the Cult of the [[Lhuunkzar|World Devourer]]) is a cult that seeks to bring about the second coming of the "[[Zchuunkzar|World Devourer]]"—a massive, worm-like creature currently sealed within the [[Void]].

This cult has followers worldwide, however the majority are centered upon the continent of [[Drastevarde]].

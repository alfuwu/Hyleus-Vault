The National Association of Magical Advancement (NAMA) is a program created by the [[Empire of Gen]] to further their understanding of all forms of magic. Today, it receives about 50 billion Jen annually from the [[Empire of Gen|Gen Empire]]’s government. Many of the brightest scholars and magicians aspire to join this program and further all species’ understanding of the fundamentals of magic.

The Monolithic Order is a fledgling organization established by [[Alfred Tonic]], [[Ezkli Reday]], [[Kate Askerō]], and [[Tarik Reday]]. Officially declared in 2652 [[HUN Calendar|ME]], it would take a further two years for the order to be acknowledged by the majority of the realms of Hyleus, in no large part due to the fact that the entire Order was centered upon just four people, none of which governed a nation of any sort.

However, each founding member possessed powerful [[intrinsic magic]] and knew how to wield it well, in addition to owning enough magical artifacts collectively to fund an entire kingdom. They used this concentrated magical strength to pose themselves as a neutral peace guarantor. Approximately 50% (100) of the survivors from [[the Fractured]], all powerful individuals in their own right, joined the Order immediately upon the announcement of its creation, bolstering its numbers and influence.

Despite the Order's youth, it is an extremely powerful organization and has rapidly grown to be militant and capable of handling strong magical monsters.

The Order is centered upon the fact that the world's fundamental responses to extraplanar threats is abysmal, and uses that fact as a foundation with which it builds up its ideology on. The recent loss of approx. 3,000,000 lives to [[the Fractured]] garnered wide public support for the Monolithic Order, which helped establish it as a tangible entity in the eyes of nations. The Order is represented by the [[Miulyn]] glyph for the number four.

The Order's command structure is as follows:
- At the top, there are the four Monoliths - the original founders of the Order. They serve as supreme power in the Monolithic Order
- Directly reporting to the Monoliths are the Cketei, a word purposefully taken from [[the Ckretion Empire]] in honor of the catastrophe the empire had suffered - the formation of [[the Abyss]]. The Cketei are handpicked by each Monolith, and are all well known. The Cketei of each Monolith are as follows:

- Each Cketei commands an army (called an Iteris, another word taken from [[the Ckretion Empire]]) of mages. Iterises are split into many Revum; military units of around 5,000-6,000 mages.
- Revum are commanded by a Revii Ackolera, and are split into ten divisions.
  
  The 1st division is the largest and comprised of the best battle mages, the 2nd and 3rd divisions are additional battle mage units, the 4th is comprised of the best non-combat mages, the 5th is a healing division and generally only has about twenty to thirty mages, the 6th-to-9th units are organized on a per-Revum basis, and the 10th unit, the smallest of them all, is a spec ops unit consisting of about eight-to-ten mages.

The Monolithic Order's command structure helps it stretch across multiple continents, though most of its influence is centered in [[Drastevarde]] and the Yiyicos.

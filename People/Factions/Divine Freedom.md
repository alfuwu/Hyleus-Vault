Divine Freedom is a now-destroyed faction of rebels seeking freedom from the parasitic divine. The group was founded by [[Meloqzykes]], who served as a charismatic leader for the cause.

At its peak, Divine Freedom had a member count in the several thousands. However, after [[Meloqzykes]] was sealed by [[Hakscles]] following the [[Divine Revolution]], in which numerous [[Deity|deities]] were slain, including [[Sethyrs]], the [[Deity|primordial deity]] of Apotheosis, the faction fractured, effectively destroyed. The aftereffects of Divine Freedom's war against deism are still felt strongly in the [[Modern Era]], particularly due to [[Sethyrs|Sethyrs's]] death—no more shall deities will rise until the Path of Apotheosis is laid bare by the [[deity]] of Apotheosis.

As [[Sethyrs]] is dead, a new deity of Apotheosis is required to take up this mantle, yet the olden methods of Apotheosis are long lost to time. [[Sethyrs]] herself was perhaps the last individual still capable of both remembering the means of primordial Apotheosis and communicating with mortals.

The many fragmented accounts of Divine Freedom's doctrines paint the picture of them believing that deities, far from being benevolent, feast upon the [[Soul|souls]] of the dead. They believed that this consumption of [[Soul|souls]] is what allowed deities their immortal strength, their source of power. They also believed that eating [[Soul|souls]] en masse would create vulnerabilities that are exploitable, potentially allowing one to kill a [[deity]]. None of these vulnerabilities were ever confirmed to exist in [[Deity|deities]], and it is unknown why the Divine Freedom ever had the idea of the [[Deity|deities]] eating [[Soul|souls]] to begin with.

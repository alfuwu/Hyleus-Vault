Divine Freedom is a now-destroyed faction of rebels seeking freedom from the parasitic divine. The group was founded by [[Meloqzykes]], who served as a charismatic leader for the cause. At its peak, Divine Freedom had a member count in the several thousands. However, after [[Meloqzykes]] was sealed by [[Hakscles]] following the [[Divine Revolution]], in which three gods were slain, including [[M]], the primordial deity of transcendence, the faction fractured, effectively destroyed. The aftereffects of Divine Freedom's war against deism are still felt strongly in the [[Modern Era]], particularly due to [[M|M's]] death—no more deities will rise until the Path of Transcendence is laid bare by the deity of transcendence, which cannot happen so long as there is no deity of transcendence.

The many fragmented accounts of Divine Freedom's doctrines paint the picture of them believing that deities, far from being benevolent, feast upon the souls of the dead. They believed that this consumption of souls is what allowed deities their immortal strength, their source of power. They also believed that eating souls en masse would create vulnerabilities that are exploitable, potentially allowing one to kill a deity.

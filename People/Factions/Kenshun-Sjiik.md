The Kenshun-Sjiik are an isolationist faction of psionics that reside on the continent of [[North Yiyico]]. They take residence in the [[National Yiyican Mountain Range]], cultivating young and aspiring psionic users. They are frequently referred to as a sort of bogeymen by the Yiyicans, due to their mystique that resulted from their isolationism. Many missing psionics have been claimed to be abducted by the Kenshun-Sjiik, though no substantial evidence has ever been uncovered.

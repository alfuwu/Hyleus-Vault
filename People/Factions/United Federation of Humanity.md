A faction that formed during the [[Rebirth Era]], the United Federation of Humanity is a relatively new xenophobic collection of nations spearheaded by the [[Great Jeienet]]. The majority of nations in this federation are democratic or oligarchic, though there are a few monarchies and autocracies within as well. This mutual defense pact has helped stabilize the world after the major wars that occurred during the [[Dark Era]].

The one universal characteristic of a nation inside this federation is that either all or the vast majority of political power is centered in human hands. For their xenophobic qualities, this federation is looked upon with distaste by many nonhuman sapients.

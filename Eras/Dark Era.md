The Dark Era was the era preceding the infamous [[Rebirth Era]], and was a time many would consider one of the worst in history. Crime rates and poverty were seen at previously unheard-of numbers, alliances and trade relations between kingdoms and empires broke down, and the global economy was suffering massively.

**Begin**: 2500 [[Astana Calendar|ME]]
**End**: 2650 [[Astana Calendar|ME]]

The Rebirth Era, directly caused by the preceding [[Dark Era]], is an era that holds the record for the most wars between different countries in 2 years, managing to total at about 9 different countries going to war with another country during the aforementioned timespan. Many kingdoms rose and fell in this era, with a few famously profiting off the turmoil, such as the [[Empire of Gen]] or the [[Great Jeienet]]. The majority of the Rebirth Era took place on [[Drastevarde]].

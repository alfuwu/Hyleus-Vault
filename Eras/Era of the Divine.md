**Begin**: 5,000 [[Astana Calendar|KE]]
**End**: 0 [[Astana Calendar|ME]]

The Era of the Divine, also referred to as the Era of Myths, is characterized by its deiocentricity. The deities were more active during this era than any other, granting piecemeal portions of divine power to chosens that intrigued them.

Historians tend to place the end of the Divine Era at about 0 [[Astana Calendar|ME]], when [[Lunærgen Sø-Bryu]] discovered [[Classical Spellcasting|classical magic]], though in truth the [[Deity|deities]] had been slowly declining in activity before [[Lunærgen Sø-Bryu|Lunærgen]] was even born, primarily due to the [[Great Hell Wars]].

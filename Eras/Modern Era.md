**Begin**: 2650 [[HUN Calendar|ME]]
**End**: Present-Future

The Modern Era, the current era, is a time of prosper and ingenuity. New ways to utilize spell matrices are being discovered, such as the famous “loop glyph set”, allowing magicians to repeatedly cast an effect in a spell over and over with just a single spell matrix, assuming the glyphs for the spell were entirely contained within the loop glyph set.

The present date as of writing this document is 2654 [[HUN Calendar|ME]].

Most modern empires use the same calendar, which started 2654 years ago. This calendar is known as the HUN (Humanity United Nations) calendar in [[English]]. It has two different states (ME, and stands for "Meryn Edselith," meaning "after epoch," and KE, standing for "Kael Edselith," which means "before epoch." Both ME and KE come from the [[Miulyn]] language.) The epoch, in this case, is when the first form of learnable magic was discovered by [[Lunærgen Sø-Bryu]], which is widely regarded as the very beginning of modern society. This calendar was formed in the year 2511 ME, during the [[Rebirth Era]].

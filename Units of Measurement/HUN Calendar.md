Most modern empires use the same calendar, which started 2654 years ago. This calendar is known as the HUN (Humanity United Nations) calendar in [[English]]. It has two different states (ME, and stands for "Meryn Edselith," meaning "after epoch," and KE, standing for "Kael Edselith," which means "before epoch." Both ME and KE come from the [[Miulyn]] language.) The epoch, in this case, is when the first form of learnable magic was discovered by [[Lunærgen Sø-Bryu]], which is widely regarded as the very beginning of modern society. This calendar was formed in the year 2511, during the [[Rebirth Era]].

Each month is made up of 4 weeks of 7 days, with 13 months in a year. Every 6 years, there is an additional day, known as "the Apex," which is traditionally celebrated with wine and pork.

The months are as follows:
- **Fedrena**
- **Atdinia**
- **Handra**
- **Gzoa**
- **Bandrosa**
- **Junali**
- **Malkun**
- **Thale**
- **Cindor**
- **Quinali**
- **Kahlow**
- **Volua**
- **Demu**

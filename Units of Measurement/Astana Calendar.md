Most modern empires use the same calendar, known as the Astana Calendar, which places the 0th year 2654 years ago. It has two different states; ME, which stands for "Meryn Edselith," meaning "after epoch;" and KE, standing for "Kael Edselith," which means "before epoch." Both ME and KE come from the [[Miulyn]] language. The epoch, in this case, is when the first form of [[classical spellcasting]] was discovered by [[Lunærgen Sø-Bryu]], which is widely regarded as the very beginning of modern society. This calendar was formed in the year 301 ME.

Each year is approximately 319 days long. There is one leap year every 1404 years.

The calendar follows a bi-annual schedule; all odd years end in summer, and all even years end in winter. This is because a *true* year on Hyleus is approximately 638 days long, and is instead split in twain to get years that are of a more reasonable length. Each year has 11 months, each 29 days in length.

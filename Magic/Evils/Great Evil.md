A Great Evil is the term used to describe the reaction that occurs when this [[plane]] crosses another in the [[Czite axis]]. These other [[Plane|planes]] are corruptive to our [[plane]] and highly deadly to all creatures living here. Once a Great Evil comes into contact with the [[mortal plane]], an anchor is established as a sort of outpost for the other reality. It is unknown if this is a natural reaction or if these anchors are purposefully sent to fulfil the purpose of spreading their respective Great Evil.

The anchors for each Evil are as follows:
- [[The Spiral]] — The [[Chronovore]]
- [[The Parasite]] — The [[Titan of Rot]]
- [[The Abyss]] — [[Oblivion]]
- [[The Starved]] — [[The Long Hunger]]
- [[The Fractured]] — The [[Storm of Eternity]]

Each one of these anchors has power on par with the level of an [[Deity|arch deity]] and keep their respective Great Evil stable. Theoretically, should one be slain, the Great Evil it protects would eventually dissipate into nothingness.

[[The Abyss]] is the only Great Evil to not stem from contact with another [[plane]], rather coming from the emptiness that lay between, known as the [[Void]].

The emergence of the Second Great Evil dates back to around 1500 [[HUN Calendar|KE]]. This great evil is known as the Parasite, and is full of rotting fauna and flora. The Second Great Evil marked a shift in public perception about these infectious lands, and led to many rightfully abandoning their belief that [[the Spiral]] was a Holy Land once a connection between [[the Spiral]] and the Parasite had been definitively proven.

The Parasite, unlike [[the Spiral]], is full of life and organic matter, albeit decaying. The true defining factor of the Parasite is its capability to spread. Any creature that dies within the Parasite becomes a part of it, their [[Soul|souls]] eternally shackled to their body and denied ascendance to the [[plane]] of [[Heaven]] yet also denied damnation to the [[plane]] of [[Hell]].

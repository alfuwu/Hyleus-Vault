The emergence of the Second [[Great Evil]] dates back to around 1500 [[Astana Calendar|KE]]. This [[Great Evil]] is known as the Parasite, and is full of rotting flora and fauna. The Parasite marked a shift in public perception about the [[Great Evil|Great Evils]], and led to many rightfully abandoning their belief that [[the Spiral]] was a Holy Land once a definitive connection between [[the Spiral]] and the Parasite was proven.

The Parasite, unlike [[the Spiral]], is full of life and organic matter, albeit decaying. The true defining factor of the Parasite is its capability to spread. Any creature that dies within the Parasite becomes a part of it, their [[Soul|souls]] eternally shackled to their body and denied the exultation of the [[Void]]. The only way to save these [[Soul|souls]] is to destroy their mortal coil.

[[Fel of the Abyss]] killed the [[Titan of Rot]] in the year 1452 [[Astana Calendar|ME]], and the Parasite has been slowly dwindling away ever since. Predictions state that the Parasite will be fully gone from the [[Mortal Plane]] by the year 2700 [[Astana Calendar|ME]].

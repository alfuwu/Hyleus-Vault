The Fourth Great Evil, known as the Starved, is a land of consumption. The entities residing there are entirely geared towards consuming more organic matter. They're so single-mindedly focused on consuming each other that they largely handle their own containment, though the constant fight for survival these creatures undergo breeds only the strongest of horrors. The slow infection rate of the Starved has led many an adventurer seeking glory to underestimate its dangers, often fatally so.

Endless Hunger.

The Fractured is the most recent [[Great Evil]] to occur, occurring at 2650 [[HUN Calendar|ME]], on the 14th of [[HUN Calendar|Demu]], and approximately 14:06 [[Drastevarden Standard Time|DST]]. The emergence of the Fractured is perhaps one of the most well-documented events in history, in part due to the amount of lives the Fractured claimed (an estimated number of 200 survivors managed to escape ground zero, out of 3,000,000) and in part due to the fact that it was a [[Great Evil]]. The Fractured has led many hypotheses to be created about these [[Great Evil|Great Evils]], such as why they solely seemed to occur within massive population centers and why they seemed to happen periodically.

Two notable survivors of ground zero of the Fractured are [[Alfred set Tonic]] and [[Ezkli Reday]], who many survivors claim were vital to getting out alive.

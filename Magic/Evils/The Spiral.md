The First Great Evil (then known as the World Evil or the Holy Land) known to exist is the Spiral. Its origins are unknown due to it existing before written records of civilization were ever made.

The Spiral's defining characteristic are the massive spirals of blue and black metallic material that populate the landscape. These spirals tower at staggering heights, many reaching as high as 200 meters into the sky, and are nigh impervious to mundane methods of damage. Thus, the Spiral has been seen time and time again to have been worshiped as a holy ground by many ancient civilizations, despite its infectious and antithetical existence. The Spiral is often regarded as the tamest of the Five Great Evils due to its lack of obvious directly damaging phenomena, however recent medical examinations has revealed that the haze which permeates the Spiral appears to cause micro-spirals to form within living creatures that breathe it in, stealing lifeblood and [[mana]] from the creature and feeding it to the Spiral.

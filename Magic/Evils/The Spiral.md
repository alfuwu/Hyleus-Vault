The First [[Great Evil]] (then known as the World Evil or the Holy Land) known to exist is the Spiral. Its exact origins are unknown due to it existing before written records of civilization were ever made.

The Spiral's defining characteristic are the massive spires of blue and black metallic material that populate the landscape. These spirals tower at staggering heights, many reaching as high as 200 meters into the sky, and are nigh impervious to mundane methods of damage. Thus, the Spiral has been seen time and time again to have been worshiped as a holy ground by many ancient civilizations, despite its infectious and antithetical existence.

The Spiral has often been regarded as the tamest of the Five [[Great Evil|Great Evils]] due to its relative lack of obvious directly damaging phenomena, however recent medical examinations has revealed that the haze which permeates the Spiral appears to cause micro-spirals to form within living creatures that breathe it in, stealing lifeblood and [[mana]] from the creature and feeding it to the Spiral. In addition, the recent "awakening" of the Spiral has firmly placed it as the second most dangerous of the [[Great Evil|Great Evils]], right under the [[The Abyss|Great Evil of Void]].

The Spiral is the only known [[Great Evil]] to have "awakened"—that is, to have experienced a drastic change for no obviously apparent reason. Many of the largest spires within the Spiral have opened up, revealing dangerous horrors that now prowl throughout the entirety of the Spiral. Where before the Spiral was only sparsely populated with these dangerous creatures, now they roam in greater numbers than even [[the Starved]] can boast. Most adventurers within the Spiral during its awakening died.

The Chronovore is a unique being with nigh absolute control over the time half of [[domain]] of spacetime while within the Spiral. Interestingly enough, the Chronovore doesn't seem to have any capability to exert its will over space, despite the [[domain]] of spacetime encompassing both space and time.

Some of the capabilities of the Chronovore include rewinding time in a localized area, changing how fast time moves in a localized area, stopping time in a small localized area. It should be noted that with a strong enough [[soul]], a person may become moderately resistant to these effects. 

The Chronovore physically appears as a massive (>100m long) mechanical serpentine creature. The metallic alloy making up the Chronovore's body is unknown.

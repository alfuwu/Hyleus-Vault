Oblivion, the anchor entity of [[the Abyss]], is not a creature, and can barely even be defined as a "being". It is more akin to a concept than a living, breathing creature. In this regard, it is quite similar to [[Ruin]], though it is noticeably distinctly different in the concept that it represents. While Oblivion represents the death, the end of all things, [[Ruin]] represents the journey something takes to reach its death. A small difference, but an important distinction nonetheless.

It is absolute, a fact that [[Fel of the Abyss]] learnt, rather unfortunately, after it consumed part of his childhood friend's [[soul]] and found he was unable to recover the missing portion of the [[soul]] by unwinding time.

Oblivion appears as a massive ball of white flame, tinged and shaded with blackness darker than the [[Void]]. All that its flames touch turn to nothingness, wiped from existence itself. This makes it an exceedingly difficult target to attack, and is also the reason why [[Fel of the Abyss|Fel]] has never attempted to re-enter [[the Abyss]] in an attempt to slay the concept in revenge for his friend.

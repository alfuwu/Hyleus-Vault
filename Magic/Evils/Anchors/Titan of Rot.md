The Titan of Rot is a unique being that lay dormant at the center of [[the Parasite]]. It towers a staggering hundred meters tall and has immense vitality. Any damage dealt to the Titan of Rot is healed within seconds, making the creature nigh immortal.

The Titan of Rot was slain in the year 1452 [[HUN Calendar|ME]] by [[Fel of the Abyss]].

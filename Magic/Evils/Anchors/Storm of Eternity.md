The Storm of Eternity is a massive storm full of scintillating shards, puppeteered around by winds generated from its core, which have been noted to go upwards of 1000 MPH. These shards are infinitely thin, capable of slicing through everything physical, up to and including ascendants. It has a much more difficult time with the [[Divinity|divine]] and incorporeal, however.

Each shard is roughly triangular in shape, with one side disproportionately elongated to form a more devastating tip. Upon contact with regular, non-magical material, it will rapidly start breaking the material down, causing it to turn into extremely fine dust.

The core of the Storm is a large spherical ball, roughly a meter in diameter, with visible fracture lines running all throughout it. These fracture lines emit a glowing white light, standing out in stark contrast to the pulsing grays and blacks of the rest of the sphere. It is surrounded by frame in the shape of an octahedron that always remains vertical. This frame rapidly rotates when physical objects or magic come near, preventing harm from coming to the core of the Storm.

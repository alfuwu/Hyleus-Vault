Authority is the innate right one has over a concept of the world. There are many different types of Authority, ranging from the Authority over the [[Domain]] of Earth to the Authority over the [[Domain]] of Time. This form of magic is not inheritable by progeny.

Authority is tied to one's [[Soul]], though the exact nature of this connection is not well understood. 

This article is a work-in-progress. More data will be added when further research has been conducted on this topic.

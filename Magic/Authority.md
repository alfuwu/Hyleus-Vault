Authority is the innate right one has over a concept of the world. There are many different types of authority, ranging from the authority over the [[domain]] of earth to the authority over the [[domain]] of spacetime itself. This form of magic is not inheritable by progeny.

Authority is tied to one's [[soul]], though the exact nature of this connection is not well understood.

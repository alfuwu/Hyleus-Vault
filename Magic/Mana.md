Mana is a force of nature unlike the other, more intuitive forces such as gravity, mass, or energy. Mana flows throughout the entire world, its origins embedded in deep space where magic and technology both fail to locate. It appears to be web-like in nature, on the grander cosmic scale, with “thin” strands moving every which way. How fortunate, then, that Hyleus be located right in the middle of one of these very strands. Analysts predict that such an occurrence, assuming that life and mana are independent of one another, had less than a 0.000003% chance of happening.

Mana comes in five distinct different forms:
- Raw
- Pure
- Residual
- Radiative
- Corrupt

Raw mana is the mana provided to the world via the web-like cosmic strand network. It must be processed by the [[soul]] into pure mana before being utilized to create [[Classical Spellcasting|spell matrices]] or manipulate magical artificery that do not absorb its own mana.

Pure mana is the mana found within the core of one's [[soul]]; this is the mana that you may use for the creation of [[Classical Spellcasting|spell matrices]].

Residual mana is mana that is left over from the creation and use of a [[Classical Spellcasting|spell matrix]], or from the leaking of a magical creature's [[aura]], mana that is ever present in our world due to the vast amounts of spells being cast each day and the magical creatures that walk the world.

Radiative mana is mana formed from the natural decay of mana formations located within the world's crust and mantle. This mana is highly volatile, though it has many valuable properties that make it extremely useful for alchemy, and, in rare cases, artifice or rituals.

Finally, corrupt mana is tainted mana. The largest contributing source of corrupt mana is the [[Void]], however it is not the only source. In extraordinary cases, a backfiring spell matrix might produce corrupt mana, and it has also been observed that [[intrinsic magic]] might naturally interact with corrupt mana depending on the nature of the [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic formation]]. The only known uses of corrupt mana, beyond destroying [[Soul|souls]], is for these forms of [[intrinsic magic]]. It is also a food source for [[Void]] creatures, which may congregate around pools of corrupted mana.

A mana particle's size is largely influenced by the amount of energy it contains. When mana purity is low and the particles are sparse, the particles will be small and the effect it has upon the world will be weak to nonexistent. On the contrary, high-power mana often results in larger particles, however this rule is not universal; it is possible to compress high-power mana particles into smaller particles, and it is also possible to expand low-power mana particles into larger particles, though both such practices have the drawback of decreasing the available power of the mana particle.

The existence of mana is the biggest contributing factor to megafauna—for mana and [[genetic magic]] allows creatures to overcome the structural limitations imposed by the physical reality they live in, bypassing the square-cubed law. This does mean, however, that the majority of large creatures rely on having a steady supply of mana to structurally support themselves, and when denied it will collapse in on itself in a rather gruesome display.

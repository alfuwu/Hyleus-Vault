Intrinsic magic is similar to [[authority]], however it is something that cannot be obtained under any reasonable circumstances past birth. Many ritualists and cultists have attempted to steal the intrinsic magic within other beings and imbue themselves with it, to no avail.

Intrinsic magic is a form of signature placed upon one's [[soul]] during their [[soul]]'s creation—a very rare circumstance of which the exact causing factors are unknown. This signature is generally very highly tied to a specific [[Domain]], again, much like [[authority]], and is capable of many effects beyond what any form of [[Classical Spellcasting|classical magic]] should theoretically be capable of. Logic-defying effects such as revival after death, shattering of the spacetime continuum in a localized area, etc. are commonplace in intrinsic magic.

Certain species have been found to have higher “chances” of forming intrinsic magic—these beings always being inherently magical in nature, such as [[Demon|demons]]. However, certain other species, such as the [[eruul'vaak]], while semi-magical in nature, have a *lower* chance of naturally being gifted intrinsic magic than nonmagical species.

The approximate chances of forming an intrinsic magic signature upon birth for each species is as follows:
- **[[Angel|Angels]]**: N/A
- **[[Deity|Deities]]**: N/A
- **[[Demon|Demons]]**: $\frac{1}{600,000}$
- **[[Eruul'vaak]]**: $\frac{1}{1,500,000}$
- **[[Fae]]**: $\frac{1}{500,000}$
- **[[Human|Humans]]**: $\frac{1}{1,000,000}$
- **[[Serran|Serrans]]**: $\frac{1}{900,000}$
- [[Tarhan]]: $\frac{1}{1,200,000}$
- [[Xe'tunig]]: N/A

Intrinsic magic has been observed to have highly increased rates of appearance in those with an ancestor who also had some form of intrinsic magic. As such, the majority of [[Human|humanity]]'s intrinsic magic is concentrated in families of noble lineage.

Deism is a broad topic. It encompasses the apotheosis of [[authority]] into something much grander and more absolute than mere [[authority]], along with all applications of the power that comes with such divine might.

Apotheosis has been studied and researched extensively over the centuries, though, after [[M]], the [[Deity]] of Apotheosis, was fractured, Apotheosis has been all but impossible. This severely limits what can be studied, as all verified cases of Apotheosis date back at least a thousand years.

Apotheosis is the process one undergoes to attain a form of divinity known as "internal divinity"—this divine power originates from you, and is the strongest form of divinity. It may be used on others to perform many reality-defying effects, though it is most often used to place permanent powerful blessings or curses. When used like in such a manner, it transforms into "external divinity"—a much more dilute form of internal divinity, making it notably weaker.

Internal divinity is difficult to recoup after spending, and as such the most common way of doing so is to forcefully take the external divinity back from wherever it was before, ending its effects. Divinity also has some notable connections to the [[Psychic Leyline]], and has been observed to, in small amounts, generate upon prayer. The exact reasons for this remain unknown, though the prevailing hypothesis claims that the [[soul]] offers up a portion of its collected [[essence]] to the [[Deity]] that the individual is praying to, where it transforms into internal divinity. The more connected an individual is to the [[Deity]] they are praying to, the larger the amount of divinity generated. As this is the only known method of *generating* internal divinity, this hypothesis strongly suggests that before Hyleus achieved the consciousness singularity threshold and developed the [[Psychic Leyline]], regular non-primordial [[Deity|deities]] did not exist, as there would be no method to gain any divinity. Many devout faithful rebuke this theory, claiming that their [[Deity|deities]] have existed since the beginning of time itself, though the [[Deity|deities]] in question have never given input on this specific question.

The path to divinity is not definite—for each person, the route to attaining deism is different. There is no catch-all method to divine power, however one condition remains constant: the individual attempting to ascend must have a massive amount of [[authority]] in whichever [[Domain]] they are attempting to ascend into. Cases of individuals attempting to ascend into a [[Domain]] that already has a [[Deity]] presiding over it have been recorded and are observed to initiate some form of spiritual battle with the preexisting [[Deity]]. There are no records of the usurper ever winning against the original [[Deity]].

Primordial forms of divinity, believed to have formed when the universe itself did, are known as [[Shard of Divinity|Shards of Divinity]]. These [[Shard of Divinity|Shards]] are the catalyst the primordial [[Deity|deities]], [[Hyleus]], [[M]], and [[Ruin]], utilized to ascend to deism. They contain massive wealths of unattributed divine power, and have been observed to be highly lethal when interacting with a [[soul]] unused to divine energies.

An interesting reaction occurs when a [[Shard of Divinity]] is placed within the body of an [[Angel]], in that, the [[Shard of Divinity]] will sever the [[Angel]]'s connection to their [[Deity]] and force them to undergo some form of Apotheosis, granting the [[Angel]] its divine energy. This attribution of primordial divine power causes the [[Angel]] to "evolve" into what is known as a “[[Angel|fallen angel]]”.

The reasons for the existence of the [[Shard of Divinity|Shards of Divinity]] is unknown. Chemical tests upon these [[Shard of Divinity|Shards]] show that they are mostly composed of carbon in a crystalline structure, similar to diamond. However, they also contain trace amounts of unknown atomic material, estimated to have a total concentration of less than 1% within the [[Shard of Divinity|Shard]], that has been dubbed “[[celestium]]”, attributed to the theory that this material is what allows these [[Shard of Divinity|Shards]] to contain divine energy without a metaphysical [[Soul]].

Deism is a broad topic. It encompasses the transcendence of [[authority]] into something much greater and more absolute than mere [[authority]], along with all applications of the power that comes with such transcendence.

Transcendence has been studied and researched extensively over the centuries, though, after [[M]], the [[deity]] of Transcendence, was fractured, transcendence has been all but impossible. This severely limits what can be studied, as all verified cases of transcendence date back at least a thousand years.

Transcendence is the process one undergoes to attain a form of divinity known as “inner divinity”—this divine power originates from you, and is the strongest form of divinity. It may be used on others to perform many reality-defying effects, though it is most often used to place permanent powerful blessings or curses. When used like this, it transforms into “outer divinity”—a much more dilute form of inner divinity, making it notably weaker.

Inner divinity is difficult to recoup after spending, the most common way of doing so being to forcefully take the outer divinity back from wherever it was before, ending its effects. Divinity also has some notable connections to the [[Psychic Leyline]], and has been observed to, in small amounts, generate upon prayer. The more connected an individual is to the [[deity]] they are praying to, the larger the amount of divinity generated. Strangely, prayers must be made in honesty—a prayer made in direct effort to specifically generate divinity will generate none. This behavior suggests that before Hyleus achieved the consciousness singularity threshold and developed the [[Psychic Leyline]], regular non-primordial [[Deity|deities]] did not exist, as there would be no method to gain any divinity. Many devout faithful rebuke this theory, claiming that their [[Deity|deities]] have existed since the beginning of time itself, though the [[Deity|deities]] in question have never given input on this specific question.

The path to divinity is not definite—for each person, the route to attaining deism is different. There is no catch-all method to divine power, however one condition remains constant: the individual attempting to ascend must have a massive amount of [[authority]] in whichever [[Domain]] they are attempting to ascend into. Cases of individuals attempting to ascend into a [[Domain]] that already has a [[deity]] presiding over it have been recorded and are observed to initiate some form of spiritual battle with the preexisting [[deity]]. There are no records of the usurper ever winning against the original [[deity]].

Primordial forms of divinity, believed to have formed when the universe itself did, are known as [[Shard of Divinity|Shards of Divinity]]. They are believed to be the catalyst the [[Deity|primordial deities]], [[Hyleus]], [[M]], and [[Ruin]], utilized to ascend to deism. These [[Shard of Divinity|Shards]] contain massive wealths of unattributed divine power, and have been observed to be lethal when interacting with a [[Soul]] unused to divine energies. An interesting reaction occurs when a [[Shard of Divinity]] comes into proximity with an [[angel]], in that, often, the [[Shard of Divinity]] will specifically seek out the [[angel]]. It is theorized that this is due to the connection the [[angel]] has to their parent [[deity]], to which the [[Shard of Divinity|Shard]] is attracted to for reasons as of yet unknown. However, upon interacting with the [[angel]], the connection to their [[deity]] severs and the [[Shard of Divinity|Shard]] instead becomes attributed to the [[angel]] itself. This attribution of primordial divine power causes the [[angel]] to "evolve" into what is known as a “[[Angel|fallen angel]]”, which is a process known as angelogenesis.

The reasons for the existence of the [[Shard of Divinity|Shards of Divinity]] is unknown. Chemical tests upon these [[Shard of Divinity|Shards]] show that they are mostly composed of carbon in a crystalline structure, similar to diamond. However, they also contain trace amounts of unknown atomic material, estimated to have a total concentration of less than 1% within the [[Shard of Divinity|Shard]], that has been dubbed “[[celestium]]”, attributed to the theory that this material is what allows these [[Shard of Divinity|Shards]] to contain divine energy without a metaphysical [[Soul]].

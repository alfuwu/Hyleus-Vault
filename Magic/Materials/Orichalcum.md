Orichalcum is a magical material that resists all external magical phenomena. This property makes it both incredibly valuable yet incredibly difficult to work with, as all magical applications of the metal are inherently resisted by the very part of the metal that makes it so valuable to begin with.

As a naturally soft element, orichalcum must be made into an alloy to be proper use. Common metals for such a process are titanium, [[mithril]], steel, and iron. However, the best metal to counter orichalcum's softness is, naturally, [[adamantium]], though [[adamantium]] is rare enough that such a combination is almost never seen. The resulting alloys are rather strong, rather mana-dense, and extremely magic-resistant. This makes orichalcum an especially valuable metal when crafting adventurer armor, though it has seen applications in weapons too, the foremost example of which being [[Lzywausya]].

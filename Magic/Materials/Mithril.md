Mithril is a metal primarily found deep within [[Miulya]], renowned for its mana-superconductive properties that make it prime material for [[Classical Spellcasting|artifice and alchemy]]. This superconductivity effect of the metal allows any [[Classical Spellcasting|enchantments]] engraved upon it to be extremely effective, operating at their maximum capacity without any hindrances. Naturally, impurities in the metal will prevent the [[Classical Spellcasting|enchantments]] from reaching true 100% efficiency, however mithril is the closest metal to 100% known to sapient society, barring [[celestium]].

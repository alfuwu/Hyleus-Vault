Ksol is an extraterrestrial element found only in a select few meteoroid impact sites, placing it almost on par with [[adamantium]] in terms of rarity. The element has shown no signs of an upper bound when it comes to [[mana]] density, absorbing, for all intents and purposes, infinite amounts of [[mana]] even in the smallest of quantities. Naturally, as there is a limit to how much energy (and thus, [[mana]]) can fit into a given mass of material, as defined by the formula $E=mc^2$, and as such, the limit to ksol's [[mana]] density must be located at some point below this limit.

The crystal's physical state is firm yet brittle; entirely unsuitable to any combat applications in its base form. As such, ksol is typically encased in some form of [[orichalcum]] to both protect it and prevent the [[mana]] contained within the element from exploding, however it is, rarely, remedied via complex and expensive [[Classical Spellcasting|alchemical]] processes, as observed in [[Rina Lutzel Tenevitale]]'s necklace.

The soul is the heart of a being—storing a vast wealth of information about them. It contains all their memories, the blueprint of their body, and any intrinsic magic formations. It is made up of several layers—the first and foremost being the surface layer (or archaically referred to as "the shield"). The surface layer protects the inner layers of the soul from scrutiny and weathers any soul attacks, shielding the more vulnerable layers of the soul from harm. Repeated soul attacks will strengthen this layer as the soul adapts, possibly causing mutations to occur within the soul, and almost certainly causing trauma to develop. Circumstances wherein the surface layer shattered are extraordinarily rare, and have all resulted in the death of the subject.

The second layer of the soul is known as the intermediate layer (or "the blueprint"). This layer contains information about who the soul is. It is theoretically possible to fully revive someone using this layer alone, however a feasible implementation of such a feat is beyond modern magic. This layer contains the blueprint of the soul's body, the soul's memories, and any [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic magic formations]]. Much of this layer gets ejected in the form of [[essence]] upon disconnection of the soul and body, which typically occurs in the form of death.

The third and final layer of the soul is the core layer (or "the well"). This layer produces mana used in [[classical spellcasting]], and sometimes used by [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic magic formations]] to fuel themselves.

The soul is partially inheritable—that is to say, it has its own set of “genetics” that may be passed onto children. For example, if you had an abnormally large pool of mana in your soul, it is likely that your children will also have an abnormally large pool of mana. However, it is not completely deterministic. It is possible that a child will develop certain characteristics within their soul by themselves, with no notable “soul genes” being passed on from any ancestor. This is known as "soul mutation". It should also be noted that [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic magic formations]] themselves are uninheritable, though the progeny of an [[intrinsic magic]] user are much more likely to have their own [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic magic formation]] within their intermediate layer. The souls of creatures with complex thought and consciousness are notably, on average, larger than the souls of creatures without. This particular correlation can be seen especially well when comparing the soul of a plant to that of an average [[human]]: the soul of the plant will be magnitudes smaller, even if the physical body of the plant is much larger than that of the [[human]]'s.

Souls slowly gather and store [[essence]] over time, using the [[essence]] to strengthen both itself and the body in four different stages: **Transcendence**, **Sublimation**, **Ascension**, and **Apotheosis**. Typical Apotheosis has been denied to the soul ever since the death of [[Sethyrs]], the [[deity]] of Apotheosis. Instead, one must undergo primordial Apotheosis if they wish to ascend to deity-hood, however the manner in which one goes about doing so has long been lost to time. The closest thing to Apotheosis in the modern day is the [[Angel]]'s capability to absorb a [[Shard of Divinity]]. Militaries tend to categorize the first three stages as Ranks I-III, with those attaining Apotheosis being classified as simply deities. Apotheosis is often regarded as a side-grade to Ascension, as, though it grants you vast amounts of power, it also layers you in vast amounts of chains and restrictions. Thus, even though there were many individuals capable of Apotheosis when [[Sethyrs]] was alive, they actively chose to not undergo it in order to preserve their precious mortality.

It is impossible to naturally view the soul or its inner workings, just as it is impossible to see one's own organs. To remedy this issue, machinery and technology have been developed to grant us a more complete insight into the soul's structure. These technologies have existed for a long time, but people have been tinkering with the soul (see [[phaawa]]) for longer still. The methods these ancient peoples used were crude, barbaric, and almost always resulted in the death of whomever was being experimented on alongside a mutilation of their soul.

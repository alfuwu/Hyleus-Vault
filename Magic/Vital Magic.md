Vital magic, often referred to as blood magic, was widely considered a black magic before the [[Industrial Era]]. It is a magic that revolves around the utilization of lifeblood instead of [[mana]], and is widely used in practice today for healing spells, one of the least evil facets of vital magic.

Vital magic encompasses many different practical applications, ranging from the aforementioned healing capabilities to blood rituals and sacrificial magic. As such, there are forms of vital magic that are prohibited by law in many countries, though notably the [[Pyx]] continent is known for its natives routinely sacrificing people and creatures using vital magic.

To use vital magic, one must first gather the lifeblood required to do so. This can be done through either extracting the vitality of another creature, whether by metaphysical means or by simply obtaining a portion of their blood, or by using the caster's own lifeblood. If one is using the blood of another creature to empower the magic, they must preserve it perfectly such that its Czite connection to the original creature's [[Soul]] is not severed. Otherwise, the blood will be no more useful for the spell than a drop of water.

For healing magic, the practitioner must inject the being they wish to heal with this lifeblood, using [[mana]] to guide the lifeblood if they lack the [[Genetic Magic|genetic]] or [[Intrinsic Magic|intrinsic]] capability to naturally manipulate lifeblood. This means that one can only heal themselves if they are using the lifeblood of another creature, which is rather impractical in mortal combat.

To perform a sacrificial ritual, one must incorporate the ritual component of [[classical spellcasting]]. Sacrificial rituals tend to be pentagrammic, using lifeblood as the medium for the ritual. The more magical the creature the blood medium is taken from, the more powerful and intense the sacrificial ritual will be. Sacrificial magic’s effects tend to empower the ritualist(s) performing the ritual, whether temporarily or permanently. To execute the sacrificial ritual, the creature the lifeblood was extracted from must be killed somewhere close to (preferably directly on top of) the ritual formation. The severing of the [[Soul]] causes a catalytical reaction to occur, sparking the ritual into motion and creating a powerful supernatural effect.
